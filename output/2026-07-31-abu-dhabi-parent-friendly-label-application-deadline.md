## Abu Dhabi Extends Deadline for Parent-Friendly Label Applications

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Early Childhood Authority (ECA) has announced an extension for the application deadline for the Parent-Friendly Label programme, allowing organisations across the UAE until August 14, 2026, to submit their applications. This decision comes following a surge of interest from various sectors, including semi-government, private, and third-sector organisations, highlighting the growing commitment to supporting working parents.

## Overview of the Parent-Friendly Label Programme

The Parent-Friendly Label is designed to recognise organisations that implement policies and practices aimed at assisting working parents in balancing their professional and family responsibilities. This year, the initiative has been expanded to include government entities as part of a pilot programme, marking a significant step in promoting family-friendly workplace environments throughout the UAE.

The expansion aligns with the UAE's national priorities for family well-being, especially as the nation observes the Year of the Family. By enhancing support for working parents, the initiative aims to create a healthier workplace culture that acknowledges and addresses the needs of families.

## Benefits of Family-Friendly Workplaces

ECA has outlined the positive impact that family-friendly policies can have on both employees and organisations. Such measures not only improve employee well-being but also serve to attract and retain talented staff, fostering a more productive and engaged workforce. The continued growth of the Parent-Friendly Label initiative reflects an increasing awareness among organisations about the importance of creating supportive environments for parents.

## Eligibility and Application Process

Organisations seeking to participate in the latest cycle of the Parent-Friendly Label must submit their applications through the dedicated website before the deadline of August 14, 2026. The programme is open to a wide range of organisations across the UAE, with specific provisions for selected government entities to participate in the pilot phase.

As the deadline approaches, the ECA encourages potential applicants to take this opportunity to enhance their workplace policies in order to support their employees, particularly parents.

## Implications for Parents and Families

The extension of the application deadline for the Parent-Friendly Label is particularly relevant for families considering their work-life balance in the UAE. As more organisations commit to family-friendly practices, parents may find increased support in their professional environments. This could lead to a more harmonious balance between work obligations and family life, which is crucial for overall family well-being.

In conclusion, the ECA's efforts to promote the Parent-Friendly Label signify a broader commitment to enhancing the quality of life for families in Abu Dhabi and beyond. As organisations adapt to these evolving standards, parents can look forward to a more supportive work environment that recognises the importance of family.

This initiative stands as a testament to the UAE's dedication to fostering a culture that values family life alongside professional commitments, ultimately contributing to stronger communities across the nation.
