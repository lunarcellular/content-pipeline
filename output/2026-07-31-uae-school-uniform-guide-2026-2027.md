## UAE School Uniform Guide for 2026-2027: What Parents Need to Know

As the new academic year approaches, the Ministry of Education in the UAE has released its official School Uniform Guide for the 2026-2027 academic year. This comprehensive resource is designed to assist parents in preparing for the first day of school, which falls on August 31, ensuring that students are dressed appropriately for their educational stage.

The guide provides detailed illustrations of approved uniform designs for students from kindergarten through Grade 12. It outlines the specific prices for each item and lists the authorised sales outlets across the country, making it easier for families to navigate back-to-school shopping.

## Key Features of the School Uniform Guide

The guide aims to reflect the UAE's national and educational values while being practical and suitable for daily school activities. By providing a single reference point, the Ministry hopes to reduce confusion for parents when purchasing uniforms. This is particularly beneficial as students across the country prepare to return to classrooms, with thousands expected to do so at the end of August.

Uniforms can be purchased at approved LuLu Hypermarket outlets and designated locations that cater to remote communities. This variety of options is intended to assist families in completing their shopping efficiently before the school year begins.

## Academic Calendar Highlights

In addition to the uniform guidelines, the academic calendar for 2026-2027 has been outlined. Key dates include:
- Winter break from December 14 to January 1
- Spring break from April 5 to April 9
- The end of the school year on July 2, 2027

These dates are crucial for parents planning their family schedules around school holidays and other commitments.
