## Introduction to Digital Attestation in the UAE

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has rolled out a fully digital service for the attestation of educational documents, significantly reducing processing times to just two hours during official working hours. This initiative is designed to streamline the process for students and graduates, enhancing access to essential documentation.

## Key Features of the New Service

The digital attestation service offers a variety of benefits aimed at both students and educational institutions. Applications can be submitted online via the ministry's official website or through a smart application, making it more convenient for users. This service encompasses a wide range of educational documents, including those issued under the Ministry of Education curriculum, international school qualifications, and other certifications from recognised educational institutions.

According to the ministry, digital documents issued in the UAE will be processed within two hours. However, documents sent through courier services may take one to three working days for delivery, depending on the chosen provider and destination. This rapid turnaround is particularly advantageous for students who require prompt access to their educational certificates for further studies or employment opportunities.

## Eligibility and Requirements

To utilise this new digital service, only UAE-issued electronic documents that include a verification feature such as a QR code, barcode, or reference number are eligible. This includes not just educational certificates but also other important documents like birth and death certificates and police clearance certificates. 

However, the ministry has emphasised that applications with inaccurate information, altered or non-original documents, or those that do not meet the specified requirements will be rejected. Once the attestation is complete, users can conveniently download their digitally attested documents from the "My Applications" section of the ministry's website.

## Implications for Students and Parents

This initiative reflects the UAE's ongoing commitment to enhancing the efficiency of its educational services. For parents and students, the digital attestation service represents a significant improvement in how educational documents are processed, reducing the time and hassle traditionally associated with document verification.
