## Overview of the 2026-2027 Academic Calendar

The UAE Ministry of Education has officially announced that the 2026-2027 academic year will feature 185 teaching days. Teachers are set to return on August 24, with students beginning classes on August 31. This new calendar applies to government [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) as well as private institutions that follow the national curriculum, offering a structured timetable for the school year.

## Structure of the Academic Year

The upcoming academic year will span 37 weeks, divided into three terms. The first term will consist of 13 weeks, while the second and third terms will each comprise 12 weeks. This structured approach aims to enhance educational planning and provide families with a clear framework for the school year.

The winter break is scheduled to start on December 14, allowing students to enjoy a holiday period. However, teaching and administrative staff will commence their break a week later, on December 21, after fulfilling their academic and administrative responsibilities. 

Following the winter recess, a spring break is planned for both students and school staff, commencing on April 5, 2027. This break allows for a mid-term rest before the academic year concludes.

## End of the Academic Year

Students will complete their academic year on July 2, 2027. Meanwhile, teachers and administrative staff will remain on duty until July 16 to complete essential tasks such as finalising examination results and preparing for the next academic year. This extended period for staff ensures that all administrative duties are effectively managed.

## Importance for Parents and Families

The introduction of this structured academic calendar is significant for parents and families as it facilitates better educational planning. With clear dates for term beginnings and breaks, families can organise their schedules accordingly, whether it involves holiday planning or childcare arrangements during school breaks.

The Ministry of Education's initiative to standardise the academic calendar across government and private schools enhances transparency in educational planning, making it easier for families to navigate the school year. This clarity is particularly beneficial in the UAE, where diverse school options and curricula can create variability in academic schedules.

In summary, the 2026-2027 academic year will be well-organised, providing a stable environment for students and teachers alike. As schools in the UAE prepare for this framework, families can look forward to a more predictable academic journey in the coming years.
