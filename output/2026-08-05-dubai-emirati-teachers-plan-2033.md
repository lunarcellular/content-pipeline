## Dubai's Renewed Focus on Emirati Teachers in Private Education

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) is on a mission to significantly enhance the presence of Emirati teachers in its private education sector, setting a goal to increase their numbers to 3,000 by the year 2033. This initiative was unveiled during the Emirati Educator Forum, organised by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA). The strategy aligns with the broader objectives of the Emirati Educator Strategy, which seeks to attract and empower national talent in the teaching profession.

The forum convened approximately 100 Emirati teachers from various private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) and early childhood centres across Dubai. It served as a platform not only for discussion but also for collaboration among educators, enabling them to share experiences and best practices. The event underscores Dubai's commitment to investing in its national talent and enhancing the quality of education within the emirate.

## Strengthening National Identity Through Education

The initiative aims to strengthen national identity within Dubai’s private education landscape while also improving the overall quality of the education ecosystem. Emirati teachers are seen as crucial in nurturing future generations and instilling national values. The KHDA emphasised that the role of these educators extends beyond traditional teaching; they are pivotal in shaping the cultural and social fabric of the community.

The forum highlighted various professional development opportunities for Emirati teachers, which are integral to the success of this strategy. Interactive workshops focused on innovative teaching methods, particularly in Arabic language instruction, and the integration of modern technology to support educational practices.

## Professional Growth and Community Building

During the forum, a strong emphasis was placed on mentorship and professional growth for Emirati educators. The KHDA reiterated its commitment to providing meaningful pathways to enhance the skills and capabilities of teachers. This aligns with the goals of the Education 33 Strategy (E33), which aims to transform the educational landscape in Dubai.

The forum not only facilitated knowledge exchange but also helped in building a professional community among educators. By connecting experienced educators with recent graduates, the initiative aims to foster a supportive environment that encourages the next generation of teachers to enter the profession with confidence.
