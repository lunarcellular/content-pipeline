## Efforts to Increase Emirati Teachers in Dubai's Private Schools

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)’s Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has launched a strategic initiative aimed at significantly increasing the number of Emirati teachers within the private education sector. The ambitious target is to elevate the count to 3,000 by the year 2033. This announcement was made during the recent Emirati Educator Forum, which gathered around 100 Emirati educators from various private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) and early childhood centres across the emirate.

The Emirati Educator Strategy, recently approved by Dubai’s Executive Council, is designed to attract and empower national talent, thereby enhancing the overall quality of education in private institutions. This initiative aligns with the broader objectives of the Education 33 Strategy (E33), which seeks to cultivate a quality education ecosystem while reinforcing national identity through effective teaching practices.

### Strengthening National Identity in Education

The presence of Emirati teachers in private schools is seen as crucial not only for educational development but also for nurturing a strong sense of national identity among students. Educators participating in the forum emphasised the importance of their roles in shaping future generations and instilling local values and traditions. The strategy underscores the belief that Emirati teachers can significantly contribute to embedding cultural knowledge and fostering a sense of belonging among students.

The forum served as a platform for professional development, allowing educators to exchange knowledge and best practices. Workshops focused on enhancing Arabic language teaching methods and integrating technology into educational practices, which are essential for modernising teaching approaches.

### Professional Development and Mentorship

KHDA is committed to creating supportive pathways for Emirati teachers, aiming to elevate their professional growth and impact within the education community. The forum included interactive sessions tailored to the specific needs of the participants, promoting mentorship and collaboration among seasoned educators and newcomers.

This initiative reflects a national commitment to invest in local educators, highlighting the significance of their contributions beyond traditional teaching roles. It aims to empower them to influence the educational landscape, ensuring that the teaching profession remains attractive to Emiratis.

### Future Implications for Parents

For parents choosing schools in Dubai, the increased focus on Emirati teachers could have meaningful implications. As the education landscape evolves, parents may find that schools increasingly prioritise hiring local talent, leading to a more culturally relevant curriculum and teaching approach that resonates with the values and traditions of the UAE.

Moreover, the commitment to enhancing the quality of education through this strategy may lead to improved student outcomes and a more enriching educational experience for children. As schools in Dubai adapt to these changes, parents will likely benefit from a more integrated educational environment that not only prepares students academically but also fosters a strong national identity.

In summary, Dubai’s strategic push to increase the number of Emirati teachers in private schools by 2033 is a significant step towards enhancing the quality of education while strengthening national identity. This initiative not only aims to empower local talent but also positions Dubai as a leading global destination for education, making it an important consideration for parents selecting schools for their children.
