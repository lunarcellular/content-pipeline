## Key Dates for the UAE School Calendar 2026-27

As the 2026-27 academic year approaches, [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the UAE are set to reopen on August 31, 2026. This date marks the beginning of the school year for most educational institutions, providing an opportunity for parents to plan ahead for term dates and holidays.

The UAE has established a unified academic calendar for public and private schools, which outlines the start and end dates of terms, as well as major holidays. This framework aims to create consistency for families, particularly when arranging vacations and childcare.

### Important Academic Dates

For the 2026-27 academic year, several significant dates are essential for parents to note:

- **First-term Mid-term Break**: Scheduled from October 12 to October 18, 2026, allowing students a brief respite in the middle of the term.
- **Winter Break**: From December 14, 2026, to January 3, 2027, this extended break spans several weeks, coinciding with the festive season.

Schools in the UAE are required to meet a minimum number of instructional days, ensuring that students receive adequate teaching throughout the year. The unified calendar framework is designed to provide greater visibility for families, allowing them to plan holidays and other commitments in advance.

### Variations in School Calendars

It is important to note that not all private schools adhere strictly to the unified calendar. For instance, schools in [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) operate under a separate regulatory framework established by the Sharjah Private Education Authority (SPEA). Despite this, many private schools in Sharjah will also commence classes on August 31, 2026.

Certain schools following alternative curricula, such as Indian, Pakistani, and Bangladeshi educational systems, may have different academic years and term dates. For example, schools offering the Indian curriculum typically start their academic year in April and conclude in March, provided they fulfil the required 182 instructional days.

Parents are advised to check directly with their child's school for specific details regarding term dates, as some institutions may have the flexibility to split mid-term breaks between October and February, ensuring that they do not exceed the total of five school days.

### Considerations for Planning

When planning for the upcoming academic year, parents should also consider expected public holidays, particularly those that are based on the Islamic calendar, which may be subject to official confirmation. Understanding these dates is crucial for effective scheduling of family holidays and other activities.

The introduction of multi-year academic calendar frameworks has been beneficial, as it allows families to prepare for key dates well in advance. This initiative is part of the UAE's ongoing commitment to enhancing transparency and predictability within the education sector.

In summary, as parents gear up for the 2026-27 school year, being aware of these key dates and understanding the variations among different curricula will be essential for smooth planning. The structured calendar not only aids in academic preparation but also helps in coordinating family commitments throughout the year.
