## Overview of A-Level Results in the UAE

In a historic academic year, students across the UAE have achieved remarkable successes in their A-Level examinations for 2026, despite facing unprecedented challenges due to the cancellation of formal exams. [Schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the region reported exceptional results, including multiple institutions achieving 100% pass rates, showcasing the resilience and dedication of students during a time of significant disruption.

## Examination Context

Due to safety concerns stemming from ongoing regional conflicts, major examination boards, including Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel, and OxfordAQA, cancelled all IGCSE and A-Level exams for the May/June 2026 series. In lieu of traditional assessments, students were evaluated through a Portfolio of Evidence, which included coursework, internal assessments, and work completed throughout the academic year.

## Outstanding Performance Across Schools

Numerous schools have celebrated their students' achievements:

- One school in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) reported that 54% of A-Level entries were awarded A*–A grades, a significant increase from previous years. Additionally, over 20% of entries received the top A* grade, while the overall pass rate reached 100%.
  
- Another institution recorded that 55% of A-Level entries received A*–A grades, with every entry achieving at least an A*–C. The year also saw a 12 percentage point increase in the proportion of A*–A grades compared to the previous year.

- A newly established institution celebrated its inaugural A-Level graduating class, with 50% of all entries receiving A*–A grades and 87% achieving A*–B grades.

- A well-known school reported that 39% of its A-Level entries were awarded A*–A grades, with 73% achieving A*–B grades, resulting in students securing places at top universities in the US and UK.

- A school in [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) also achieved recognition, with 37% of its A-Level entries awarded A*–A grades and 71% achieving A*–B grades, reinforcing the academic excellence of UAE students.
