## KHDA's New Policies for Dubai School Fees

As [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) prepare to reopen on August 31, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has clarified key regulations regarding school fees. These rules, which govern application fees, registration deposits, and refund processes, are crucial for parents navigating the financial aspects of enrolling their children in private education.

### Key Fee Structures for Parents

Under the new regulations, schools can charge a maximum application fee of Dh500, which is not refundable if a place is offered and the parent chooses not to accept it. However, if a child is not offered a place after the assessment, this fee must be refunded. 

Parents are required to pay a registration deposit of up to 10% of annual tuition fees only after they have received and accepted a formal offer of admission. This deposit is deducted from the child's tuition fees once they commence their school journey. Importantly, the deposit is refundable if the parent provides a 60-day written notice before the child's start date.

For existing students, a re-registration deposit of up to 5% of tuition fees or Dh500, whichever is lower, can be charged. This fee secures a place for the following academic year and must also be deducted from the upcoming year's tuition fees. 

### Refund Deadlines and Conditions

The KHDA has established clear deadlines for refund eligibility. Parents must keep in mind two critical timelines:

1. **60-Day Notice**: Written notice must be submitted at least 60 calendar days before the child's start date to receive a full refund of the registration deposit. This applies similarly to re-registration deposits for the next academic year.
   
2. **10-Day Window**: If a KHDA-approved fee increase occurs after payment, parents have a 10-day window to withdraw their child and qualify for a full refund. This refund is calculated based on when the school receives the formal withdrawal request, not on the child's last attendance day.

If a child withdraws after the school year has commenced, the retained fees depend on the duration of attendance:
- **Two weeks or less**: One month’s fees may be charged.
- **More than two weeks but up to one month**: Two months’ fees may be charged.
- **More than one month**: No refund is granted, and full term fees are charged.

Should a child withdraw before the academic year starts, fees are typically refundable, although the deposit might be retained if the 60-day notice was not provided.

### Compensation for Service Interruptions

In circumstances where a school cannot provide education due to suspension or government-mandated closures, parents are entitled to compensation for the duration of the service disruption. This compensation can be issued as a credit note valid for six months, a transferable credit for a sibling at the same school, or a full refund. However, if the school continues to provide educational services through alternative approved arrangements, fees remain payable, even if parents choose not to utilise them.

### Importance for Parents Navigating School Fees

The new policies from KHDA are designed to provide clarity and protect parents financially. By understanding these fee structures and deadlines, parents can make informed decisions regarding their children's education. The emphasis on transparency in school fees and refund processes is particularly relevant in a diverse educational landscape where financial planning is essential.

As the new academic year approaches, this guidance from KHDA reassures parents that they have rights regarding fees and refunds, ultimately allowing for a smoother transition into the school year ahead. Understanding these policies can alleviate financial concerns and help families focus on the academic success of their children.
