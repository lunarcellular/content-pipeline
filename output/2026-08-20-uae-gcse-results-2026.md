## Strong Performance in Challenging Times

UAE students have achieved remarkable success in their GCSE results for 2026, recording high proportions of top grades despite disruptions caused by regional conflicts. This year, major UK exam boards, including Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel, cancelled all formal International GCSE and A-level examinations in the UAE for the May/June series due to safety concerns. Nevertheless, students from various British curriculum [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) demonstrated resilience and dedication, achieving outstanding academic performance.

## Record Results Amidst Adversity

The results released this week reveal that over 4,250 students from several British curriculum schools in the UAE received results for approximately 30,000 entries. Of these, 30% of grades were at the highest levels of 9–8/A*, while nearly half, at 48%, achieved grades of 9–7/A*–A. Notably, students were awarded a total of 4,177 Grade 9s, highlighting the exceptional capabilities of the student cohort.

In comparison to the national average in England from 2025, where only 12% of entries received grades 9–8/A*, the performance of students in the UAE showcases a significant distinction. Moreover, the percentage of entries achieving grades 9–8/A* increased from 26% in 2025 to 30% this year, indicating a positive trend in academic outcomes.

## Highlights from Various Institutions

Several schools reported impressive results, with some students achieving straight 9s across all subjects, placing them among the highest achievers nationally. For instance, one school reported that 81% of all grades awarded were within the 9–6 range, achieving a 100% pass rate across all subjects. Another institution had a total of 744 exam entries, with 53% achieving grades 9–8/A*–A, and a remarkable 99% achieving grades 9–4/A*–C.

Additionally, a school with a cohort of 122 students noted that 100% were entered for examinations, with 21% of entries achieving Grade 9. Furthermore, 97% of entries achieved grades 9–4/A*–C, reflecting a strong overall pass rate.

Across the wider curriculum, one institution celebrated its best GCSE results to date, with Grade 9 accounting for 43% of all grades. Overall, 60% of grades were awarded at 9–8, with a significant majority of students achieving grades 9–7 across various subjects.
