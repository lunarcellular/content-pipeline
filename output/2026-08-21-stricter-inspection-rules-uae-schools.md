## Overview of New Inspection Rules

As the new academic year approaches, [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in the UAE will face enhanced inspection requirements aimed at bolstering safety and student protection. The Ministry of Education has outlined comprehensive guidelines that all government schools must adhere to by the 2026-2027 academic year. This initiative underscores a commitment to providing safe and conducive learning environments for students.

## Key Aspects of the Inspection Framework

The newly established inspection framework encompasses multiple critical areas, including building safety, transportation standards, canteen operations, and overall student welfare. Specific requirements mandate that schools ensure their facilities are free from defects, with particular attention to walls, ceilings, and floors. Safe movement within the premises is also a priority, necessitating unobstructed corridors and staircases.

### Safety and Surveillance Measures

To enhance security, schools are required to implement internal and external surveillance systems while maintaining student privacy. These measures are designed to protect pupils and ensure their safety throughout the school day. Additionally, schools must have adequate first-aid provisions and protocols in place to safeguard health and wellbeing.

### Transportation Regulations

Transportation safety has been a focal point of the new rules. Schools must comply with existing transport regulations applicable in their respective emirates, ensuring that bus drivers and supervisors are appropriately licensed. Clear monitoring procedures for students using school transport are also mandatory, enhancing accountability and safety for pupils commuting to and from school.

### Food Safety in School Canteens

Canteens are subject to stringent health and safety regulations, with all food sold needing prior approval from relevant authorities. Schools must maintain proper storage conditions to keep food at appropriate temperatures and organise sales points to prevent overcrowding. Furthermore, staff involved in food service must be trained and licensed to uphold food safety standards.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

The new regulations place significant emphasis on inclusion and support for pupils of determination and those with additional educational needs. Schools are mandated to provide accessible environments and tailored support programmes, ensuring that these students receive the necessary assistance without exclusion from enrolment.

## Preparing for the New Academic Year

As administrative, teaching, and technical staff prepare to return to schools, the first week of the new term will focus on meetings and workshops aimed at curriculum reviews and teacher development. The academic calendar indicates that students will commence their studies on August 31, following a week of preparations for staff.

The first term will also include various assessments, such as Arabic baseline assessments and diagnostic testing, with winter break scheduled to begin for pupils on December 14 and for staff on December 21.

## Implications for Parents

For parents choosing schools in the UAE, these stricter inspection rules signify a heightened commitment to safety and quality in education. The focus on safety regulations, health standards, and student support ensures that schools prioritise a secure learning environment, catering to the diverse needs of all students. Parents can feel more assured that their children will be in facilities that adhere to rigorous standards of care and safety.

In summary, as the UAE gears up for the new academic year, the implementation of these comprehensive inspection rules reflects an ongoing effort to enhance educational quality and safeguard students, ultimately contributing to a robust educational framework across the country.
