## Introduction to New Transport Measures

As families in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) prepare for the upcoming academic year, significant improvements in school transport options and safety measures have been announced. These initiatives are aimed at ensuring that student journeys are not only safer but also more reliable and convenient for parents.

## Enhanced Transport Options

The Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) has revealed plans to expand the variety of transport services available to students, complementing traditional school bus offerings. This includes collaboration with e-hail limousine operators to provide dedicated services tailored for school commutes. Notable options will include ride-hailing services that employ safety-rated drivers and modern vehicle fleets, enhancing the overall travel experience for students.

Additionally, a new ride-pooling initiative in Al Barsha will allow students to share rides, which is expected to provide a cost-effective alternative for families. This service will be available through a dedicated app, facilitating easier coordination of school trips among families.

## Safety Measures and Inspections

In tandem with the expansion of transport options, the RTA has underscored the importance of safety by implementing stricter checks for school transport operators. A circular has been distributed to ensure that all school buses are fully operational and compliant with safety regulations. This includes comprehensive inspections of vehicles and verification of driver and attendant permits before the commencement of services.

A 30-day intensive inspection campaign will be introduced, focusing on ensuring compliance with safety standards across all school buses in Dubai. This initiative aims to monitor adherence to operating procedures and enhance safety measures for students during their commutes.

## Public Transport Enhancements

To further support families, the RTA has announced an increase in the capacity and frequency of public transport services around school zones. More than 30 bus routes serving key school areas will see an uptick in service frequency, aimed at reducing waiting times and improving overall accessibility for students commuting to and from [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools).

For those using public transport, students with a nol Card will benefit from a 50 per cent discount on various RTA services, including the Metro, Tram, and public buses. This initiative is designed to encourage the use of public transport and provide financial relief to families.

## Increased Taxi Availability

Recognising the heightened demand during peak school hours, the RTA will also increase taxi availability in several school zones. This adjustment aims to cater to the influx of students and parents during school opening and closing times, making the transport experience smoother and more efficient.

## Conclusion: What This Means for Parents

The expansion of school transport options and the emphasis on safety are significant steps forward for families in Dubai as they navigate the complexities of school commutes. With increased choices and enhanced safety measures, parents can feel more assured about their children's journeys to and from school. These developments reflect a commitment to improving the educational ecosystem in Dubai, making school transportation a priority in the overall strategy for the 2026-27 academic year.
