## Overview of Dubai's Education Expansion

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private education sector is poised for significant growth, with plans to open 26 new educational institutions in the 2026-27 academic year. This expansion includes seven new [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), 17 early childhood centres, and two higher education institutions, collectively providing nearly 17,000 new places for students. This development reflects the ongoing commitment to enhance educational options for families across the emirate.

## Growth of Private Education in Dubai

The expansion of Dubai's education sector comes at a time when it has recorded an annual growth rate of 4.6% over the last three academic years. This growth persisted even amidst challenges faced during the 2025-26 academic year, highlighting the resilience and continued investment in the education sector by various providers.

### New School Offerings

Among the new institutions set to open, the seven schools will offer a diverse mix of curricula. Four will follow the UK curriculum, while three will provide the International Baccalaureate programme. This aligns with the emirate's strategy to enhance the variety of educational offerings available to families.

The introduction of these schools follows an increase of 21 new educational institutions in the past three years, which has seen enrolment grow by 3.1%. The ongoing demand for quality education options underscores the confidence families place in the local education system.

## Expansion of Early Childhood Centres

In addition to new schools, 17 early childhood centres (ECCs) will be established, adding more than 1,700 places for early learners. These centres will provide a variety of curricula, including the UK's Early Years Foundation Stage, Montessori, Ontario, French, and Indian programmes. This expansion reflects a broader commitment to improving access to early education, which is crucial for child development.

Over the past three years, Dubai has seen the opening of 87 new ECCs, with enrolment in this sector growing by 2.6%. The increasing focus on early childhood education highlights the importance placed on foundational learning in the emirate.

## Higher Education Developments

The higher education landscape in Dubai is also evolving, with the planned opening of two internationally recognised institutions. The Peking University HSBC Business School, ranked 47th globally, will launch MBA and EMBA programmes in January 2027, while the American University of Beirut's Dubai campus is set to open in September 2026. This addition brings the total number of higher education institutions opened in Dubai over the past three years to 11, with enrolment in this sector increasing by 29%.

## Implications for Parents and Students

The expansion of Dubai's education sector presents numerous benefits for families seeking educational opportunities for their children. With the introduction of new schools and centres, parents will have greater flexibility in selecting educational institutions that align with their values and educational goals. The variety in curricula offered will cater to different learning preferences, ensuring that families can choose the best fit for their children's needs.

Furthermore, the increase in the number of educators, which has grown by 17.5% over the past three academic years, indicates a strengthening of the teaching workforce in Dubai. This influx of qualified teachers is essential to maintain high educational standards and improve student outcomes.

Overall, the anticipated growth in Dubai's education sector not only enhances the choices available to families but also aligns with broader economic goals aimed at establishing Dubai as a leading global education hub. Parents can look forward to a more diverse and enriched educational landscape in the coming years.
