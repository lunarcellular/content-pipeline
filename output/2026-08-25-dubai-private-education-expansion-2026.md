## Expansion of Dubai's Private Education Sector

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private education sector is poised for significant growth, with plans to open 26 new institutions in the 2026-27 academic year. This development includes 17 early childhood centres, seven [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), and two higher education institutions, collectively aimed at increasing educational opportunities for families across the emirate.

The introduction of these new schools will add nearly 17,000 seats for students, while the early childhood centres will provide more than 1,700 additional places. The expansion is a response to the ongoing demand for diverse educational options, particularly in a city that has witnessed a steady growth in its private education sector, which recorded an annual increase of 4.6% over the past three years.

## Diverse Educational Offerings

Among the seven new schools, four are expected to follow the UK curriculum, while three will offer the International Baccalaureate programme. This variety aims to cater to the diverse needs of families in Dubai, enhancing the educational landscape and providing more tailored learning paths for students.

In addition to the new schools, the early childhood sector will see 17 new centres introduced, offering a mix of curricula, including the UK's Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS), Montessori, Ontario, French, and Indian programmes. The growth of early childhood education is critical as it lays the foundation for lifelong learning.

Over the last three years, the private education sector in Dubai has already welcomed 21 new schools, with overall enrolment increasing by 3.1%. The early childhood segment has also seen a rise, with 87 new centres opening during the same period and enrolment growing by 2.6%.

## Higher Education Advancements

The expansion isn't limited to primary and secondary education. Dubai's higher education landscape is also set to benefit, with the anticipated launch of MBA and EMBA programmes by a globally ranked university in January 2027, and the opening of a new campus of a prominent Lebanese university scheduled for September 2026. Over the last three years, 11 new higher education institutions have opened, leading to a remarkable 29% growth in enrolment in this sector.

## Growth in Teacher Workforce

The expansion of educational institutions has been accompanied by a significant increase in the teaching workforce. The number of teachers in Dubai's private education sector has risen by 17.5% over the past three academic years, reflecting the growing demand for quality education and the need for qualified educators to support the increasing student population.

The Director General of the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) described the opening of new centres and institutions as essential for providing learners with a broader range of choices at every educational stage. This aligns with the objectives of the Education 33 Strategy, which aims to attract international institutions and equip learners with the necessary skills and values for future challenges.

## Implications for Families

For parents in Dubai, the expansion of the private education sector translates to more choices and opportunities for their children. With new schools and centres catering to various curricula and educational philosophies, families can select institutions that best align with their values and educational goals. This diversification not only enhances competition among schools but also drives improvements in educational quality across the board.

As Dubai continues to grow as a global educational hub, the ongoing investments in the education sector signal a commitment to nurturing the next generation of learners. Families can look forward to an enriched educational environment that prioritises accessibility, quality, and diversity.
