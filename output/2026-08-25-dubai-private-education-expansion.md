## Major Expansion in Dubai's Private Education Sector

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private education sector is poised for significant growth in the 2026-27 academic year, with 26 new institutions scheduled to open across the emirate. This expansion includes 17 early childhood centres, seven [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), and two higher education institutions, reflecting the ongoing demand for diverse educational options.

### Increased Capacity for Students

The introduction of these new institutions will provide nearly 17,000 additional seats in schools and over 1,700 places in early childhood centres, enhancing educational opportunities for families in the region. The new schools will cater to a variety of curricula, with four adopting the UK curriculum and three offering the International Baccalaureate programme. This diversity allows families to select a school that aligns with their educational preferences.

Over the past three years, Dubai's private school sector has already seen the addition of 21 new schools, leading to a 3.1% increase in student enrolment. The early childhood sector has also expanded, with 87 new centres opening, resulting in a 2.6% growth in enrolment during the same period.

### Growth in Higher Education Institutions

The higher education sector is also thriving, with plans for the opening of the Peking University HSBC Business School in January 2027, which will offer MBA and EMBA programmes. Additionally, the American University of Beirut is set to commence operations in Dubai in September 2026. Over the last three years, 11 new higher education institutions have launched, contributing to a remarkable 29% increase in enrolment.

### Workforce Expansion

The number of teachers in Dubai's private education sector has increased by 17.5% in the past three academic years, ensuring that the growing student population is supported by a robust and qualified workforce. This growth in teaching staff is crucial for maintaining educational quality as the sector expands.

### Strategic Goals and Future Outlook

The expansion aligns with the Dubai Economic Agenda, known as D33, and the Education 33 Strategy. These initiatives aim to broaden educational choices, attract international institutions, and equip learners with essential skills and knowledge for the future. The Director General of the local education authority has emphasised the importance of every new institution in enhancing the educational landscape for learners at all stages.

For families in Dubai, this expansion of the private education sector represents a positive shift, providing more options and potentially increasing competition among schools, which could lead to enhancements in educational quality and affordability. Parents can look forward to a broader array of choices that cater to diverse educational needs and preferences, ensuring that their children receive the best possible education.

Overall, as the private education sector continues to grow, families in Dubai will benefit from increased access to quality education, better facilities, and a variety of curricular options that reflect the city's multicultural environment.
