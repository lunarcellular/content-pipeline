## Abu Dhabi Schools Held Fully Accountable for Student Transport Safety

In a significant move aimed at enhancing student safety, the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) has established comprehensive regulations mandating that [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are fully responsible for the safety of students during transport. This policy, effective immediately, requires schools to either provide their own transport services or engage external operators to ensure compliance with safety standards.

### Key Regulations and Responsibilities 

Under the new guidelines, schools must deliver necessary support and assistance for students with additional educational needs, allowing them to benefit fully from school transport services. Should there be exceptional cases where transport cannot be provided, schools are required to seek an exemption from the Integrated Transport Centre (ITC).

ADEK has underscored the importance of safety by stating that schools will bear complete accountability for all students and individuals aboard school buses, regardless of whether these services are outsourced to third-party operators. This marks a crucial step in ensuring that the welfare of students is prioritised across the educational sector.

### Violations and Penalties 

In a bid to enforce these new standards, ADEK has introduced a stringent framework for monitoring compliance, identifying 56 types of violations associated with school transport. These violations are categorised into those applicable to schools, transport operators, drivers, and bus supervisors.

Penalties for infractions can be severe, ranging from written warnings to fines that can escalate to Dh200,000 for repeated violations. For instance, schools failing to provide a sufficient number of buses may face an initial warning followed by substantial fines for subsequent infractions. Other violations include not appointing a school transport officer or failing to provide necessary bus supervisors for younger students.

### Implications for Parents and Schools

This enhanced accountability framework not only tightens regulations for schools but also serves as a reassurance to parents concerned about student safety during transport. With these measures, parents can expect a higher standard of care for their children while commuting to and from school.

The proactive stance taken by ADEK reflects a broader commitment to improving educational standards and ensuring that all students, particularly those with additional needs, receive the support they require. As schools adapt to these regulations, parents should remain informed about their child's school transport arrangements and any measures being put in place to comply with the new guidelines.

### Conclusion

The introduction of stringent regulations concerning school transport in Abu Dhabi represents a significant advancement in the safety and welfare of students. By holding schools accountable and implementing a detailed system of fines and corrective measures, ADEK aims to foster a safer educational environment. As these changes take effect, they will undoubtedly influence how schools operate their transport services, ultimately benefiting the entire community. Parents are encouraged to engage with schools regarding their transport policies and the measures being taken to ensure student safety.
