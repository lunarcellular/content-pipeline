## Overview of New Curriculum Updates

The Ministry of Education has approved a significant overhaul of the school curriculum set to take effect in the 2026-2027 academic year. These changes aim to enhance educational outcomes across various subjects and academic tracks, thereby impacting how students will learn in the UAE.

## Key Changes to Subjects and Curriculum Structure

One of the most notable changes involves the restructuring of science courses for several grades, particularly in the General Education track for Grades 9 and 10, and physics for Grades 10 to 12 in the Advanced track. As part of these updates, optional subjects for Grade 11 students have been phased out, leading to unified study pathways and a reduction in elective options for Grade 12 students.

The changes extend to language instruction as well. For instance, science for Grade 9 students in the Advanced track will now be taught in English instead of Arabic, reflecting a broader shift towards English-language instruction in certain subjects.

In mathematics, the curriculum for Grade 12 has been aligned across both General and Applied tracks, while the sequence of content for Grade 5 mathematics has been restructured to support progressive learning. Additionally, the number of weekly mathematics periods for Grades 3 and 4 has been reduced from seven to six.

## Discontinuation and New Subject Integration

Among the curriculum updates is the discontinuation of Chinese language instruction in kindergarten and Grades 1 and 2. The Ministry has indicated that this decision is part of wider revisions to kindergarten subjects, which also include updates to student learning outcomes.

Significantly, the Ministry has merged the existing Artificial Intelligence curriculum with Design, Innovation and Computer Science, creating a new subject titled “Artificial Intelligence and Technology.” This integration aims to align with the National Artificial Intelligence Curriculum Framework and is expected to enhance students' technological skills.

## Enhancements in Learning Materials and Assessment Tools

To support these curriculum changes, the Ministry has developed comprehensive guides for Grades 9, 10, and 11, along with updated laboratory guidelines and assessment criteria covering Grades 1 through 12. New assessment tools have also been introduced to evaluate students' learning levels from Grade 4 up to Grade 10 in the General track.

These enhancements are designed to ensure that students acquire the necessary skills and knowledge progressively, preparing them for future academic challenges.

## Implications for Parents and Students

For parents, these curriculum changes represent a substantial shift in how their children will be educated in the coming years. The move towards more integrated subjects, particularly in technology and language instruction, indicates a growing emphasis on equipping students with relevant skills for the future job market.

Moreover, the reduction of elective options and the restructuring of existing courses may influence parents' choices when selecting [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) for their children. Understanding the implications of these curriculum updates will be essential for parents navigating their children’s educational pathways in the UAE.

As schools prepare to implement these changes, staying informed about the specific adjustments in various subjects will be crucial for ensuring that children receive the best possible education in line with the new curriculum.
