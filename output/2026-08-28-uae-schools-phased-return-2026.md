## Phased Return of Students in UAE Schools

As the new academic year approaches, students in UAE [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) will return in a phased manner, a strategy outlined by the Ministry of Education. The organised transition, which runs from August 31 to September 3, 2026, aims to facilitate a smooth reintegration of students before full attendance resumes on Friday, September 4.

### Details of the Phased Return

The phased return allows individual schools to determine which year groups attend on specific days, with the option for schools to adjust schedules based on their readiness and needs. During this period, students will follow reduced hours to acclimatise gradually to the school environment. The Ministry’s framework is designed to ensure continuity in teaching and to prepare for the full return of students.

Schools must establish five teams to oversee various aspects of the return process: management, activities, parent communication, reception and support, and security and safety. Each team has specific responsibilities, including preparing school facilities, managing communication with families, and ensuring student safety during the transition.

For example, the activities team will be responsible for preparing event areas and ensuring that all safety equipment is in place. Meanwhile, the parent communication team will focus on sending out positive messages to families, supporting a welcoming atmosphere.

### Attendance Schedules

The schedule for attending school will vary by grade level. Kindergarten students will attend from 8:15 am to 11:30 am from Monday to Thursday and from 8:00 am to 11:30 am on Fridays. Cycle 1 students have two options: they can either attend from 7:15 am to 11:15 am or from 8:15 am to 12:15 pm during the phased days. 

For Cycle 2 and Cycle 3, boys will attend from 7:15 am to 12:15 pm during the phased period, while girls will attend from 8:15 am to 1:15 pm. All students are expected to return to school under normal schedules starting September 4.

### Importance of the Phased Approach

This phased return is crucial for several reasons. It allows schools to manage logistics effectively, ensure safety protocols are in place, and address any issues that may arise as students return. The Ministry of Education has emphasised the importance of assessing the return process, seeking feedback from parents and students to improve future transitions.

For parents, this structured approach offers reassurance as their children return to the classroom environment. The flexibility provided to schools to adjust their schedules according to specific needs is significant, particularly in light of the ongoing adjustments in educational settings across the UAE.

### Conclusion

As the new academic year begins, the phased return strategy marks a thoughtful approach by the Ministry of Education to ensure that students in UAE schools are welcomed back safely and effectively. Parents can expect communication from schools regarding specific arrangements as the return dates approach, thereby aiding in a smooth transition for families and students alike.
