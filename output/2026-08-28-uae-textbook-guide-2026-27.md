## Overview of the New Textbook Guide

The UAE Ministry of Education has introduced a new guide that outlines the textbooks and learning resources for students for the academic year 2026-27. This initiative is designed to provide families and [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) with a clear reference for the educational materials required at each grade level, supporting the overall objective of enhancing the quality of education in line with UAE Vision 2031.

## Key Components of the Textbook Package

The Student Curriculum Book Package Guide identifies three main components for each student’s learning package. These include printed textbooks, educational resources and learning kits, as well as electronic books available through the Smart Learning Platform. This model effectively combines both printed and digital materials, creating a comprehensive learning package tailored to each grade.

### Textbook Distribution Process

The Ministry has established a four-stage process for the distribution of textbooks. Initially, a contracted company will deliver the necessary books to schools across the UAE according to an approved timetable. Schools are then responsible for verifying that the quantities supplied match the actual enrolment numbers at each educational stage. The subsequent stages involve formal approval of delivery and receipt documents with the distributor, followed by the final distribution of books to students based on the approved packages for each grade.

## Managing Textbook Loss and Damage

The guide also addresses the management of lost or damaged textbooks, distinguishing between accidental and deliberate damage. If a textbook is lost or damaged unintentionally, students must submit a formal request to the school administration, which will evaluate the circumstances and facilitate a replacement. In instances of deliberate damage or loss, replacements will be ordered from the approved supplier at the student's expense.

## Importance of the New Guide

This new guide is a crucial step towards creating an integrated learning environment, ensuring that students have access to a complete and approved curriculum. By establishing clear guidelines for textbook distribution and maintenance, the Ministry aims to strengthen the awareness of the importance of printed educational resources among students, parents, and school administrations.

### Accessing the Textbook Lists

The first-term textbook lists for the 2026-27 academic year are categorised into three groups. The first list pertains exclusively to electronic books, while the second and third lists include both printed and electronic books for [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) and the Northern Emirates, and for [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) respectively. Parents, students, and schools can easily access these approved lists through a QR code included in the guide.

## Implications for Parents and Schools

For parents navigating the educational landscape in the UAE, this comprehensive textbook guide brings clarity and structure to the resources their children will need in the upcoming academic year. The integration of both physical and digital materials provides flexibility and supports diverse learning styles. Furthermore, knowing the procedures for managing textbooks ensures that families are fully informed about their responsibilities in maintaining educational materials.

Overall, the introduction of this guide reflects a commitment to improving educational standards and accessibility across the UAE, ultimately benefiting students' academic experiences.
