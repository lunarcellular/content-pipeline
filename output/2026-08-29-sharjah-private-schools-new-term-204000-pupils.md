## Introduction to the New Term

[Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah)'s private education sector is gearing up for the new academic year, which begins on August 31, 2026. The emirate is set to welcome more than 204,000 pupils across its private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), alongside the opening of six new campuses, further enhancing educational opportunities for families in the region.

## Expansion of Private Education

The Sharjah Private Education Authority has confirmed that the new schools will increase the total number of licensed private educational institutions in the emirate to 135. This expansion aims to broaden the curriculum options available to families, with three of the new schools offering the Indian curriculum and three following the American curriculum. 

In addition to the new schools, the authority has licensed seven new [nurseries](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries), bringing the total number of early childhood centres to 161. This move is part of a broader strategy to enhance educational provision for younger children and provide families with more choices in early childhood education.

## School Preparedness and Safety Measures

As part of the preparations for the new term, the authority conducted 132 field inspections to ensure that schools meet necessary educational and operational standards. These inspections included evaluations of school readiness and safety measures for transportation. A total of 2,737 school buses have been cleared for operation, adhering to approved safety standards, which is crucial for ensuring the wellbeing of students as they commute to school.

## Professional Development for Educators

Professional development has also been a focus in the lead-up to the new academic year. The authority reported that 321 training courses have been provided for teaching and administrative staff, aimed at enhancing their professional skills and overall performance within private schools. In a bid to boost the national presence within the education sector, an additional 114 Emirati teachers have been recruited through a dedicated programme designed to foster long-term career pathways for local educators.

## Strengthening School-Family Relationships

The authority has launched a campaign titled "My School, My Family," which aims to strengthen the relationship between schools and families. This initiative encourages collaboration between educators and parents to support pupils' academic and personal development. The campaign reflects a commitment to fostering a learning environment where academic achievement goes hand-in-hand with personal growth and value development.

## Conclusion and Implications for Families

The start of the new term marks a significant moment for the private education landscape in Sharjah, presenting parents with a broader range of options and ensuring that schools are well-prepared to welcome students. The growth in enrollment and the introduction of new educational facilities underscore the emirate's commitment to enhancing educational standards and accessibility. Families can look forward to a more diverse educational experience as the new term unfolds, with increased support for both students and educators alike. 

This expansion and focus on quality education not only represent a response to rising demand but also a proactive approach to shaping the future of education in Sharjah. As the new academic year begins, parents will be keenly aware of their choices, considering both curriculum options and the quality of facilities available to their children.
