## Abu Dhabi School Transport: 10,000 Buses Ready for Students

As the new academic year approaches, [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) has announced a significant enhancement to its school transport infrastructure, preparing a fleet of over 10,000 school buses set to serve more than 279,000 students. This initiative aims to improve safety and manage the increased traffic associated with the return of pupils to [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in the emirate.

### Traffic Management Measures

To facilitate smoother traffic flow during peak school hours, authorities have implemented a temporary restriction on heavy vehicles and buses entering Abu Dhabi Island from 6:30 AM to 9:00 AM. This measure, effective from August 31 to September 4, is designed to alleviate congestion and ensure student safety during the busy morning commute. While general logistics vehicles are restricted, those providing essential services, such as cleaning companies, are exempt.

### Safety and Compliance Checks

Abu Dhabi Mobility has ensured that all school buses meet stringent technical and safety requirements through comprehensive inspections. This proactive approach includes rigorous training for bus drivers, supervisors, and transport coordinators to ensure compliance with safety regulations. The authority has emphasised that school bus operators must only employ drivers who possess the required professional permits, which include criteria such as a minimum age of 25 and a valid UAE driving licence held for at least one year.

### Enhancements in Parental Communication

The introduction of the Salama school transport app is a noteworthy advancement for parental engagement. This app enables parents to track their children’s bus journeys in real time, providing updates on when their children board and disembark, as well as estimated arrival times. The app has gained significant traction, with over 126,000 downloads, enhancing transparency and communication between schools and families regarding daily transport.

### Stringent Driver Regulations

Abu Dhabi Mobility has outlined specific regulations that drivers must adhere to, reinforcing the importance of student safety. Key requirements include ensuring no child is left on the bus after the journey, prohibiting the use of mobile phones while driving, and adhering to posted speed limits, with a maximum speed limit of 100 km/h strictly enforced.

### Implications for Parents

This comprehensive approach to school transport is crucial for parents considering educational options for their children in Abu Dhabi. The availability of a large fleet of well-regulated buses not only addresses safety concerns but also indicates a commitment by authorities to enhance the overall educational experience. Parents can feel more assured knowing their children are transported under strict safety guidelines, making it easier to focus on their academic progress rather than transport logistics.

As the academic year begins, the concerted efforts of Abu Dhabi's transport authorities reflect an ongoing dedication to improving educational environments through effective and safe transport solutions. This initiative not only enhances student safety but also signifies a broader commitment to the quality of education and student welfare in the emirate.
