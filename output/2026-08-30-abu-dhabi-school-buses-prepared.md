## Abu Dhabi's School Transport Strategy

As the new academic year approaches, [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) is set to ensure the safe transportation of over 279,000 pupils with a fleet of more than 10,000 licensed school buses. This initiative is part of a broader strategy implemented by the Integrated Transport Centre (Abu Dhabi Mobility), aimed at easing traffic congestion and enhancing safety during peak school hours.

### Traffic Management Measures

To support this transportation effort, there will be restrictions on heavy vehicles and buses from entering Abu Dhabi Island during morning rush hours. Specifically, these restrictions will apply from 6:30 AM to 9:00 AM from Monday to Friday, running from August 31 to September 4. This measure is crucial as it coincides with the return of students and the expected increase in school-related traffic.

### Bus Fleet Readiness and Safety Protocols

The extensive bus fleet has undergone comprehensive checks to meet technical and safety requirements outlined by Abu Dhabi Mobility. These inspections form part of an operational plan designed to ensure that all buses are fit for safe transport. Alongside the vehicle checks, there is a focus on training for bus drivers, supervisors, and transport coordinators to maintain high safety standards.

To bolster parental oversight, Abu Dhabi Mobility has encouraged the use of the Salama school transport app. This digital platform allows parents to track their children's bus journeys in real time, providing updates on boarding, disembarking, and estimated arrival times. Since its launch, the app has been downloaded over 126,000 times, reflecting a significant engagement from the community.

### Safety Regulations for Drivers

Abu Dhabi Mobility has established stringent regulations for school bus drivers to ensure student safety. These include the requirement for drivers to be at least 25 years old, possess a valid UAE driving licence for a minimum of one year, and have completed approved training. Additionally, drivers must provide a recent good-conduct certificate and have no significant traffic violations.

The regulations extend to operational protocols, prohibiting drivers from allowing children to disembark at unauthorised stops without written instructions from the school. Measures are also in place to ensure that no child is left on the bus at the end of a journey, reinforcing the commitment to student safety.

### Implications for Parents

For parents in Abu Dhabi, the preparations for the new school year signify a proactive approach to enhancing the safety and reliability of school transport. The availability of a well-regulated bus fleet, alongside digital tracking capabilities, allows families to feel more secure about their children's daily commutes.

As the academic year begins, these initiatives not only aim to streamline transportation but also enhance the overall experience for students and parents alike. The focus on safety and efficiency is expected to set a positive tone for the return to school, ensuring that both parents and students can navigate the new school year with confidence.
