## New Educational Landmark in Dubai

A notable addition to [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s educational landscape is the opening of a new premium school, which is set to welcome its first cohort of pupils this academic year. The school aims to replicate the ethos and traditions of its prestigious UK counterpart, offering a unique educational experience that aspires to meet the needs of modern learners.

## Campus Features and Capacity

Located in Jumeirah Village Circle, the school occupies an expansive five-hectare site, designed to accommodate up to 2,000 students. The facilities include state-of-the-art science laboratories, a two-storey dining hall, a sports hall with an indoor swimming pool, as well as grass rugby pitches and an auditorium. Such extensive amenities are intended to support a comprehensive educational experience, combining academic excellence with extracurricular engagement.

## Tuition Fees and Accessibility

For parents considering this option, tuition fees for the upcoming academic year range significantly based on the grade level. For instance, fees start at Dh80,000 for Foundation Stage 1 and increase to Dh106,000 by Year 6. As the school expands to include older grades, the fees are expected to rise to Dh128,000 for Year 13. This pricing structure reflects the premium nature of the institution, which is part of a broader trend of high-end educational offerings in Dubai.
