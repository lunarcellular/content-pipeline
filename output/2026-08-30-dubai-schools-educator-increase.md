## Significant Increase in Educators in Dubai Schools

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) have welcomed back over 8,000 educators for the 2026-27 academic year, marking a notable 17.5% increase in the number of teachers working within the emirate's private education sector over the past three years. This surge highlights Dubai's commitment to enhancing its educational landscape and reflects a strategic focus on the pivotal role educators play in shaping the future of learning.

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) hosted a series of virtual town halls aimed at fostering community among educators. These sessions attracted both returning and new teachers, emphasising the importance of collaboration in achieving the objectives outlined in the Education 33 (E33) Strategy. This strategy aims to equip students with the necessary skills, knowledge, and confidence to thrive in an increasingly complex world.

## The Role of Educators in Dubai's Education Strategy

KHDA has underscored that the success of education reforms cannot solely rely on policies and strategies; it is the educators who bring these plans to fruition in classrooms. The authority highlighted that teaching extends beyond mere lesson delivery, significantly influencing student confidence, character, and aspirations. This holistic approach to education is integral to fostering a supportive learning environment.

The new academic year also marks the establishment of the Dubai Educators Council, comprising 15 members who will provide a platform for educators to voice their insights and contribute to shaping educational policies. This initiative aligns with KHDA's broader commitment to community involvement and collaboration within the educational framework.
