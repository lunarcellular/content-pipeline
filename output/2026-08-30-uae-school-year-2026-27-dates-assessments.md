## Overview of the 2026-27 Academic Calendar

The academic year for public and private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) following the Ministry of Education curriculum in the UAE is set to begin on August 31, 2026, lasting for 37 weeks. This school year will encompass a total of 185 teaching days, divided into three terms, each with specific assessment schedules and breaks.

## Term Breakdown

The school year will be structured as follows:

- **First Term**: Spanning 13 weeks with 67 teaching days.
- **Second Term**: Lasting 12 weeks with 60 teaching days.
- **Third Term**: Also 12 weeks, concluding with 58 teaching days.

The academic year is scheduled to end on July 2, 2027. 

## Assessment Schedule

Assessments will commence shortly after the students return to class, with key dates including:

- **Arabic Baseline Assessment (ABA)**: September 7 to October 2.
- **Diagnostic Examinations**: September 7 to 18.
- **Standardised Proficiency Assessment (SPA)**: October 5 to 30.

Students can expect a mid-term break from October 12 to 16, during which administrative and teaching staff will participate in specialised training.

### November Testing Intensification

In November, testing will ramp up with mock exams taking place from November 18 to 20, followed by the first round of end-of-term examinations scheduled from November 25 to 27. After the National Day holiday from December 2 to 4, exams will continue from December 7 to 11.

## Holiday Breaks

The winter break for pupils will begin on December 14 and extend through January 1, while make-up examinations for the first term will occur from December 14 to 18. Teachers and administrative staff will have their winter break starting December 21.

Pupils and staff will return for the second term on January 4, 2027, with first-term results anticipated between January 6 and 8.

### Second Term Assessments

The second term will feature an Eid Al Fitr break from March 8 to 12, followed by school-based final assessments from March 22 to April 2. The spring break is set from April 5 to 9, with students and staff returning on April 12.

## Final Term and Examinations

The final term will include significant assessment periods, including the main TIMSS 2027 study scheduled from April 19 to May 14, and the Standardised Proficiency Assessment running from May 3 to June 11. Mock examinations will take place from June 14 to 18, concluding with end-of-year exams from June 21 to 30.

The academic year officially concludes for students on July 2, 2027. Make-up examinations will be held from July 1 to 6, with final results expected to be announced from July 7 to 9.

## Professional Development for Staff

Throughout the academic year, there will be multiple opportunities for specialised training for teachers and administrative staff. These sessions are structured to occur before the school year begins and continue in October, December, and July, ensuring staff are equipped with the necessary skills and knowledge to support student learning effectively.

## Conclusion

The academic calendar for the 2026-27 school year provides a clear framework for families and schools, offering a structured timeline for teaching days, holidays, and assessments. With the Ministry of Education's commitment to a three-year academic schedule, families can plan with greater certainty, making informed decisions about their children's education in the UAE. Understanding these key dates and assessment schedules is essential for parents navigating the educational landscape.
