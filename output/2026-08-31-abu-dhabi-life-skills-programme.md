## Introduction to the Life Skills Programme

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Family Care Authority (FCA) has launched a comprehensive Life Skills Programme aimed at children and teenagers aged seven to 18. This initiative, in collaboration with the Ministry of Education, is designed to enhance essential skills that help young people navigate daily challenges and prepare for future responsibilities. The programme will run until the end of the year and is set to take place across Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, and Al Dhafra.

## Objectives of the Programme

The Life Skills Programme aims to equip participants with practical skills that are crucial for personal and social development. Workshops will cover a wide range of topics including money management, public speaking, online safety, and emotional regulation. These interactive sessions are intended to engage young individuals in a way that promotes both learning and practical application of the skills discussed.

### Key Workshop Topics

Participants in the programme will have the opportunity to engage in eight different training workshops, focusing on various life skills such as:

- Rights and responsibilities
- Assertive communication
- Systematic thinking
- Banking services
- Conflict resolution
- Managing feelings of frustration
- Online awareness and safety

In addition to these topics, the workshops will also address leadership, emotional intelligence, social responsibility, Emirati values and etiquette, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and positive thinking. The structure of the workshops, which combines theoretical knowledge with practical activities, ensures that participants can apply what they learn in their daily lives.

## Importance of Life Skills

The development of life skills is increasingly recognised as a vital aspect of education. By fostering these skills from an early age, young people can build confidence and improve their ability to interact with peers, make informed decisions, and take responsibility for their actions. This focus on life skills not only prepares them for various situations but also contributes to the formation of healthy relationships and effective coping mechanisms.

### Insights from Education Authorities

Education authorities in Abu Dhabi have emphasised the significance of integrating life skills into the educational framework. They believe that experiential learning, which includes discussion and participation, can significantly enhance a child’s understanding of themselves and their surroundings. This approach aims to empower students to express their views and handle different situations with increased confidence.

## Community and Parental Involvement

The programme is designed to strengthen collaboration between families, [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), and community organisations. This cooperative effort is crucial in supporting the development of children and young people, ensuring that they are well-equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills as they grow.

This initiative aligns with broader efforts to prepare youth for the complexities of modern life, equipping them with both practical skills and essential values.
