## Introduction to the Life Skills Programme

[Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) has launched an innovative life skills programme aimed at children and young people aged seven to 18. This initiative, running until the end of the year, is designed to equip participants with essential skills such as money management, public speaking, online safety, and emotional regulation. The programme is a collaborative effort between the Family Care Authority and the Ministry of Education, targeting the holistic development of youth in the emirate.

## Workshop Details and Curriculum

The life skills programme offers a series of eight interactive workshops that cover a range of topics vital for personal and social development. These include:

- **Rights and Responsibilities**: Understanding legal and personal rights.
- **Assertive Communication**: Techniques to express thoughts and feelings confidently.
- **Systematic Thinking**: Approaching problems methodically.
- **Banking Services**: Basics of financial management and banking.
- **Public Speaking**: Skills for effective communication in public settings.
- **Conflict Resolution**: Strategies to resolve disputes amicably.
- **Managing Feelings of Frustration**: Techniques to handle emotions positively.
- **Online Awareness and Safety**: Navigating the digital world securely.

Participants will engage in practical activities that complement the theoretical aspects of these workshops, ensuring they can apply the skills learned in real-life situations.

## Objectives of the Programme

The overarching aim of this initiative is to foster personal and social skills among youth, enhancing their confidence and decision-making abilities. The Family Care Authority emphasised that developing life skills during formative years is crucial for children's ability to navigate various challenges and build healthy relationships. The programme also seeks to strengthen the partnership between families, educational institutions, and social organisations, promoting a community approach to youth development.

## Importance of Life Skills in Education

Life skills are increasingly recognised as essential components of education. They go beyond traditional academic learning, preparing students for the complexities of modern life. According to the Assistant Undersecretary for School Operations at the Ministry of Education, such programmes provide valuable opportunities for experiential learning, enabling young people to better understand themselves and express their views confidently.

By integrating life skills training into educational curricula, Abu Dhabi aims to create a generation of well-rounded individuals who are not only academically proficient but also socially responsible and capable of thriving in diverse environments.
