## New British Curriculum School in Dubai Welcomes Students

In a significant development for the educational landscape in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai), a new British curriculum school has opened its doors, marking the first UAE branch of a prestigious UK state school. This milestone institution welcomed its inaugural cohort of 350 pupils, signalling a growing demand for British education in the region.

The newly launched school is a branch of an illustrious grammar school that was established by royal charter in 1573 in the UK. This expansion into Dubai represents its first international outpost. The school has undergone substantial refurbishment to accommodate students from [nursery](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries) through Year 8, with annual fees ranging from Dh93,000 to Dh136,604.

## Educational Philosophy and Class Size

The school's approach to education is informed by its UK counterpart, emphasising a culture of scholarship and a strong sense of community. To ensure personalised attention, class sizes are limited; Foundation Stage classes are capped at 16 pupils, while the maximum class size across the institution is set at 20. This structure aims to foster an environment where every child is known and supported.

Each class up to Year 6 benefits from the presence of a dedicated teaching assistant, contributing to a nurturing educational atmosphere. The teaching staff averages 12 years of experience, many having previously opened [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), reinforcing the institution's commitment to quality education.

## Facilities and Partnerships

The school boasts a range of impressive facilities, including access to outdoor sports pitches and indoor sports amenities through a 30-year partnership with a local sports entity. Additional offerings include an esports lab, which aligns with the school's vision of competing in robotics championships in the coming years.

Moreover, a collaboration with a renowned music college will provide students with masterclasses and performance opportunities. A health and wellness coordinator will also be introduced to oversee nutrition, mental health, and wellbeing programmes for the school community.

The establishment of a sensory room for children with additional needs highlights the institution's commitment to inclusivity and support for diverse learning requirements.

## Growing Demand for British Education

The opening of this school is part of a broader trend in the UAE, where the demand for British curriculum schools continues to rise. This trend is attributed to a mix of British expatriates and an increasing number of non-British and Emirati families seeking the structure and global recognition offered by the British education system.

Education experts have noted that in a competitive market like Dubai, the right school must address not only academic excellence but also the emotional and social needs of students. The presence of this new school adds to the existing array of British-curriculum schools, further enriching the educational options available to families.

As the educational landscape evolves, parents in Dubai can expect more choices that cater to a variety of needs and preferences, ensuring that children receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for the future. 

This expansion of British curriculum schools not only reflects the preferences of families but also the broader commitment of the UAE to enhance its educational offerings, making it a prime destination for quality education in the region.
