## New Ethical Standards for Teachers in Abu Dhabi Schools

[Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are now mandated to enforce a comprehensive code of professional conduct for teachers and staff, which aims to uphold national values and ensure a safe educational environment. The guidelines, introduced by the Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK), cover critical areas such as classroom behaviour, professional relationships, and the responsible use of technology, particularly artificial intelligence.

### Key Elements of the Code of Conduct

The new regulations require all schools to develop their own staff codes of conduct that align with ADEK's Professional Code of Ethics Policy. This initiative is designed to foster an environment of integrity, dignity, and ethical behaviour among educators. Notably, the code applies to all private and charter schools within the emirate, extending its reach to staff, school governing boards, and owners.

One of the central tenets of the new guidelines is the commitment to respect the UAE's national identity and cultural values. Educators are expected to treat all students and colleagues fairly, regardless of their background, and to maintain a discrimination-free atmosphere. This includes ensuring equitable treatment for individuals with special educational needs or disabilities.

### Prohibited Conduct

The code explicitly outlines a range of prohibited behaviours, including discrimination, harassment, and any actions that could undermine the integrity of the educational environment. Staff are barred from engaging in conduct that promotes extremism or bullying, and they must adhere to appropriate dress codes that reflect cultural sensitivity. Moreover, educators are not permitted to provide paid private tutoring to students enrolled at their own schools, which aims to prevent conflicts of interest.

### Emphasis on Digital Conduct

Given the increasing role of technology in education, the code also addresses digital conduct. Educators are tasked with guiding students on how to evaluate the reliability of online information and the ethical use of artificial intelligence. They must also monitor for instances of cyberbullying and plagiarism, ensuring that students understand these issues and know how to respond appropriately.

Teachers are required to undergo continuous professional development to keep abreast of best practices in digital safety and information security. This proactive approach aims to equip educators with the necessary skills to navigate the complexities of modern educational environments.

### Reporting Obligations and Community Involvement

The code establishes strict reporting obligations for educators regarding any suspected abuse or maltreatment of students. Failure to report such incidents is considered a serious breach of professional standards. Additionally, educators are encouraged to participate in community and sustainability initiatives, fostering a spirit of civic responsibility among students.

ADEK has made it clear that schools must ensure compliance with these new ethical standards, with a phased implementation beginning in the 2024-25 academic year, and full compliance expected by the start of the 2025-26 academic year. Non-compliance could result in legal repercussions for schools under ADEK regulations.

### Implications for Parents

For parents considering educational options for their children in Abu Dhabi, these new guidelines signify a robust commitment to maintaining ethical standards within schools. The emphasis on respect for cultural values, student safety, and the responsible use of technology is likely to enhance the educational experience for students. Parents can feel reassured that educators are held to high professional standards, which are crucial for fostering a supportive and enriching learning environment.

As the educational landscape in Abu Dhabi continues to evolve, these measures represent a significant step towards ensuring that schools not only provide quality education but also uphold the values and integrity essential for the development of future generations.
