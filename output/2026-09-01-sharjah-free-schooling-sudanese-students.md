## Sharjah Offers Free Schooling for Sudanese Students

In a significant initiative, the Ruler of [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) has ordered that Sudanese students be admitted to a local school without any tuition fees. This directive aims to support Sudanese families facing financial difficulties, ensuring their children can continue their education without interruption.

The directive, communicated through a local radio programme, mandates the refund of any nominal fees that may have been collected from parents during the current academic year. This decision is particularly relevant as many Sudanese families are experiencing exceptional challenges that hinder their ability to pay for education.

### Context of Free Education for Sudanese Students

The decision to provide free schooling aligns with the establishment of a school dedicated to the Sudanese community, which was founded under the Ruler's instructions. The initiative underscores a commitment to educational access for all children, regardless of their families' economic situation.
