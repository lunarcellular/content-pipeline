## Important Fee Refund for Sudanese Students in Sharjah

In a significant move to ensure educational accessibility, the Ruler of [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) has mandated that no fees be collected from Sudanese students attending a designated private school. This directive aims to uphold the original intention behind the establishment of the school, which was to support families in the Sudanese community who face financial challenges.

The Sharjah Private Education Authority (SPEA) has been instructed not to charge any fees, including those previously labelled as nominal. The Ruler clarified that even minimal fees can be burdensome for families in need. This decision follows a complaint from a parent indicating that the school had begun requesting tuition fees, contrary to its original purpose of providing free education to Sudanese children in the emirate.

### Background of the School's Establishment

The private school was founded to serve the Sudanese community, created with the intent of providing education without financial constraints. The Ruler emphasised his direct oversight of the school’s operations, ensuring that educational standards and student welfare are prioritised. He also noted that the school was meant to relieve the financial pressure on Sudanese families, who may struggle to afford tuition at other institutions.

### Immediate Actions and Future Implications

The instruction to refund previously collected fees reflects a broader commitment to ensuring that education remains accessible to all families, regardless of their economic situation. The Ruler's proactive stance also highlights the importance of community-focused educational initiatives, aiming to foster an environment where all children have the opportunity to learn without financial barriers.

This development is particularly relevant for parents navigating the landscape of school fees in Sharjah and the wider UAE. With rising tuition costs in private education, such measures can provide reassurance to families concerned about affording quality education for their children.
