## Overview of New Timings

The Ministry of Education has confirmed the official school timings for the 2026-27 academic year, which will commence on September 7. This new schedule details the start and dismissal times for students and teaching staff across [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in the UAE.

## Detailed Timings for Various Educational Stages

The approved schedule establishes different timings based on educational stages and gender. For instance, the school day will start as early as 7:15 am for certain stages, while the latest dismissal time will be set at 3:15 pm for girls in the second and third cycles from Monday to Thursday. 

### Kindergarten Schedule

For kindergarten students, the school day will begin at 7:30 am, with dismissal at 1:00 pm. The daily structure includes morning activities and a five-lesson format, with each lesson lasting 45 minutes from Monday to Thursday. On Fridays, kindergarten students will have a shorter day, concluding with a dismissal period designed for storytelling.

### Cycle 1 Timing Options

Cycle 1 students will have two timing options. The first option includes a morning assembly starting at 7:15 am, with the first lesson at 7:25 am. This option features seven lessons throughout the day. Alternatively, the second option has a later start, with the morning assembly beginning at 8:00 am and the first lesson at 8:15 am, concluding the day at 2:20 pm.

### Timings for Cycles 2 and 3

For boys in the second and third cycles, the school day will also commence at 7:15 am, with the final lesson finishing at 2:15 pm. In contrast, girls in these cycles will start their day at 8:00 am, concluding at 3:15 pm. Both groups will have structured break times during their lessons.

## Adjustments for Friday School Days

Fridays will have shorter school hours across all cycles, with adjustments made to accommodate the shorter lesson durations. For example, kindergarten students will have a total of 24 lessons per week, while Cycle 1 students will attend 32 lessons. Students in Cycles 2 and 3 will have a heavier workload, with 36 lessons weekly.

## Implications for Parents

These new timings are significant for parents as they will directly impact daily routines, transportation logistics, and extracurricular activities. Understanding these timings will help parents effectively plan their children’s schedules for the upcoming academic year.
