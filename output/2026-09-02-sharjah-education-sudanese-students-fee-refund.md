## Sharjah Ruler Mandates Fee Refund for Sudanese Students

In a significant move, the Ruler of [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) has ordered the immediate cessation of all fees for Sudanese pupils enrolled at a local school and mandated refunds for any fees previously collected. This directive addresses concerns raised by families who were unexpectedly charged tuition, despite the school being established as a free educational option for the Sudanese community.

The initiative stems from the Ruler’s recognition that many Sudanese families face financial hardships, particularly since the ongoing conflict in Sudan began in 2023. The school was originally founded to support these families, ensuring children had access to education without the burden of tuition fees. The ruler stated that any fees, even nominal, could pose a significant financial strain on the families served by this institution.

## Context of the Directive

The education authority had initially introduced minor fees, which were intended to cover operational costs. However, this decision came into question after parents reported that their children’s certificates were withheld due to outstanding fees, contrary to the school’s intended purpose of being a free educational resource. The Ruler has expressed a commitment to overseeing the school’s operations closely, ensuring accountability and proper support for both students and educators.

This decision not only reflects the Ruler’s direct involvement in educational matters but also highlights the UAE’s broader commitment to humanitarian support for the Sudanese population amidst current crises. Since the start of the conflict, the UAE has taken various measures to assist Sudanese nationals, including allowing residency visa renewals for those in the country and providing humanitarian aid to displaced individuals.
