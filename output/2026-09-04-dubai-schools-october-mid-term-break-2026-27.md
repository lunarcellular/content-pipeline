## Dubai Schools Adjust October Mid-Term Break Dates for 2026-27

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) have announced a revision to the October mid-term break dates for the academic year 2026-27. The new dates, which have been communicated to parents, shift the break from October 19-23 to October 12-16, with classes resuming on Monday, October 19, 2026. This adjustment means that students will return to school one week earlier than previously planned.

This change aligns with the academic calendar approved earlier this year by the Ministry of Education, which governs both public and private educational institutions. The Ministry's calendar, released in February, outlines the school term dates for the next three academic years, from 2026 to 2029. The mid-term break is part of a broader effort to standardise school schedules across the UAE, ensuring that families have consistent dates to plan around.

## Importance of Adhering to the Unified Academic Calendar

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has reiterated the necessity for all private schools in Dubai to comply with the unified academic calendar established by the Ministry of Education. In an official statement, KHDA emphasised that schools should clearly communicate these calendar changes to their communities, ensuring transparency and clarity for parents and students alike.

This requirement for alignment is crucial, particularly for families with children in different educational systems, as it helps to avoid confusion and conflict in scheduling. It is important to note that this new calendar does not impact schools following alternative curricula—such as Indian, Bangladeshi, or Pakistani systems—which may have their own distinct academic schedules.

## Implications for Parents and Students

For parents, these adjustments to the mid-term break may offer both challenges and opportunities. With the break starting earlier, families may find it easier to plan vacations or family gatherings without conflicting with school days. However, the revised timing may also require adjustments in childcare or family schedules, particularly for those who had planned around the previous dates.

As the new academic year begins, parents should remain vigilant about updates from their children's schools regarding any further changes or announcements. The requirement for schools to adhere to a unified calendar is intended to streamline the educational experience across Dubai, fostering a more cohesive community for families navigating the complexities of school schedules.

In conclusion, the revised October mid-term break dates for Dubai schools reflect a significant effort to standardise the academic calendar in alignment with the Ministry of Education's guidelines. As families adapt to these changes, clear communication from schools will be essential to ensure a smooth transition for all stakeholders involved.
