## Dubai Schools Adjust Mid-Term Break Dates

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) have revised their October mid-term break dates for the 2026-27 academic year, in line with the Ministry of Education's (MOE) newly issued academic calendar. The revised break will take place from October 12 to October 16, 2026, with classes resuming on October 19, 2026. This adjustment moves the break forward by one week from the previously scheduled dates of October 19 to October 23, 2026.

### Context of the Revision

The new academic year has commenced for many schools in Dubai, which has prompted this change in the mid-term break schedule. The Ministry of Education had previously announced the school dates for the next three academic years, spanning from 2026 to 2029. The MOE's directive mandates that all private schools in Dubai adhere to this unified academic calendar.

According to guidance from the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), it is essential for private schools in Dubai to align their academic calendars with the official dates set by the MOE. KHDA emphasised that schools should ensure the updated calendars are clearly communicated to their communities.

### Implications for Different Curricula

It is important to note that this revised academic calendar does not extend to all private schools. Schools that follow alternative curricula, such as those based on Indian, Bangladeshi, or Pakistani systems, may have different academic years and term breaks. These schools are not required to comply with the MOE's unified academic calendar and often establish their own schedules based on their specific educational frameworks.

### What This Means for Parents

For parents navigating the education landscape in Dubai, these changes underscore the importance of staying informed about school schedules. The adjustment to the mid-term break dates may affect family plans, especially for those who have already made arrangements based on the previous calendar. Parents are encouraged to check with their respective schools for any additional updates or changes to the academic schedule, particularly as the academic year progresses.

This development highlights the ongoing efforts by education authorities in the UAE to standardise the school calendar, which aims to facilitate a more cohesive educational environment across the various curricula offered in the country. As the academic landscape evolves, parents should remain proactive in understanding the implications of these changes on their children’s education.
