## Changes to October Break Dates in UAE Schools

Several [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the UAE have announced a shift in their October half-term break, moving it one week earlier than in previous years. The new break will now occur from **October 12 to October 16**, aligning with the unified academic calendar established by the Ministry of Education and the Knowledge and Human Development Authority, which regulates private schools in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai).

This change has been communicated to parents and students through official notices. The adjustment means that the break will now take place from **Monday, October 12 to Friday, October 16**, rather than the previously scheduled dates of **October 19 to October 23**. This modification reflects an effort to standardise the academic calendar across the UAE, as local authorities were given the discretion to make adjustments based on operational needs.

## Impact on Families and Students

While the standardisation of the academic calendar aims to create consistency, it has led to some discontent among parents. Many families had already made plans around the original October break dates, including travel and holiday arrangements. This sudden change has resulted in frustration for those who must now reconsider their plans or seek potential refunds for bookings.

For students preparing for critical exams, particularly in British curriculum schools, the new dates present additional challenges. Reports indicate that students in **Year 13**, who will be facing significant assessments during this period, may feel the impact of this altered schedule, particularly as they will not benefit from a break during a demanding time in their academic calendar.

## Communication Gaps Among Schools

Interestingly, not all schools have communicated the new break dates effectively. Reports from families in [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) indicate that some have not received any notification regarding the change, leading to uncertainty about whether a half-term break will occur at all. This lack of clarity is particularly concerning in Sharjah, where parents have expressed worry that the shortened school week could further complicate their children's educational progress if breaks disrupt learning continuity.

## The Broader Context of the Academic Calendar

The Ministry of Education had previously announced the academic calendar for both public and private schools, establishing a framework for the next three years. The spring break is also set to begin on **October 12**, but schools have the flexibility to adjust terms as needed. This autonomy allows educational institutions to tailor their academic schedules to better fit their operational requirements, but it also requires clear communication to avoid conflicts and confusion among families.

As the educational landscape in the UAE continues to evolve, parents are advised to stay informed about any changes to academic calendars, particularly as schools navigate the complexities of aligning with government guidelines while also addressing the needs of their communities.

In conclusion, while the adjustment to the October break may provide a more standardised approach to the academic calendar, it underscores the importance of communication between schools and families.
