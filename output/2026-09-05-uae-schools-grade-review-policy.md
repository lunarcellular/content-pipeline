## New Review Policy for Student Grades in UAE Schools

The Ministry of Education in the UAE has announced a comprehensive new grading policy that will enforce four levels of review and moderation for student grades, starting from the 2026-2027 academic year. This initiative aims to enhance accuracy, fairness, and consistency in the assessment of student performance across [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools).

Under this new assessment framework, the grading process will begin at the classroom level with the individual teacher. The responsibility will then progress through the school principal, the school cluster director, and finally culminate at the Ministry’s General Education Examinations and Assessment Department. This tiered approach is designed to ensure that grading does not rely solely on the judgment of individual educators or institutions, thereby mitigating discrepancies in how student performance is evaluated.

## Objectives of the New Grading System

The Ministry of Education has outlined that the new policy is focused on preventing variations in grading standards across different classrooms and schools. It mandates that schools conduct thorough moderation checks across all grades and subjects. These checks are intended to verify that teachers apply consistent assessment standards and marking procedures.

To facilitate this, schools will be required to organise regular grade-moderation sessions. These sessions will create formal opportunities for teachers to review assessment practices, discuss marking standards, and ensure that student work is evaluated consistently. This collaborative approach will not only help in standardising assessment criteria but also foster professional discussions among educators to align their understanding of grading.

## Strengthening Credibility in Student Assessment

The implementation of this four-level review system is part of a broader strategy to strengthen the credibility of student grades in the UAE education system. By systematically identifying and addressing any variations in assessment before they become embedded in final results, the Ministry aims to uphold the integrity of academic evaluations. The policy reflects an understanding of the complexities involved in educational assessment and the necessity for a structured approach to ensure fairness.

Additionally, the School Operations Sector teams will conduct field visits to schools within the same cluster to assess the application of consistent standards of quality, accuracy, and fairness in grading practices. This oversight is crucial for maintaining a high level of educational integrity across the board.

## Implications for Parents and Educators

For parents, this new grading policy signifies a commitment from the UAE government to enhance the educational experience for their children. It assures that students will be assessed more equitably, which can help instil confidence in the educational qualifications earned. With this systematic approach to grading, parents may feel more secure in the knowledge that their children's academic achievements are reflective of their true capabilities.

Educators will also benefit from this structured system as it promotes collaboration and supports professional development. By engaging in regular discussions about assessment criteria, teachers can enhance their skills and ensure that they are equipped to provide accurate and fair evaluations of student work.

In conclusion, the introduction of a four-level grade review process marks a significant advancement in the UAE's educational assessment landscape. It is a proactive step towards ensuring that all students receive fair and consistent evaluations, ultimately contributing to a more robust educational framework in the UAE.
