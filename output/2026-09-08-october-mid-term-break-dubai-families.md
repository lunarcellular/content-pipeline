## Changes to the October Mid-Term School Break

The recent adjustment to the October mid-term school break has left many families in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) scrambling to revise their holiday plans. This year, several private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) have moved the break forward by one week, now scheduled from October 12 to October 16, 2026. Classes will resume on Monday, October 19, 2026, compared to the previous schedule which had the break set for October 19 to October 23, 2026.

This decision aligns with the unified academic calendar set forth by the UAE's Ministry of Education (MOE), which was announced earlier this year in February. The new academic calendar, covering the years 2026 to 2029, mandates these mid-term dates for private schools in Dubai, as confirmed by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA).

## Impact on Family Travel Plans

As a direct result of this change, many parents have found themselves needing to adjust travel arrangements, with some opting for staycations or local trips instead. Families who had previously booked flights and accommodations around the original dates are now faced with unexpected expenses and logistical changes to their plans.

For instance, one family expressed that they needed to rebook their flights after the dates were altered, while others have cited the need to reconsider their plans for a longer holiday. With a shorter gap following the summer break, many parents are likely to choose activities closer to home rather than organising another significant trip abroad.

## Flexibility in Family Plans

Interestingly, some families have reported that the revised break actually suits their needs better. Adjustments to the mid-term dates have enabled certain parents to attend family events without conflicting with their children's school schedules. This has provided a silver lining for those who find the changes beneficial, easing the pressure of having to coordinate school attendance with family commitments.

## Adherence to the Unified Academic Calendar

The KHDA has reiterated that all private schools in Dubai must adhere to the academic calendar established by the MOE. This requirement is crucial for maintaining consistency and clarity among families regarding school schedules. However, it is worth noting that schools following alternative curricula, such as Indian or Pakistani systems, may not be bound by these dates and can set their own schedules.

The shift in the mid-term break highlights the importance of effective communication between schools and families. Schools are expected to keep parents informed about any changes that may affect their plans, ensuring that families can adapt to new schedules with minimal disruption.

## Conclusion

As the October mid-term school break approaches, Dubai families are beginning to navigate the challenges posed by the recent calendar changes. While some parents are having to adjust their travel plans significantly, others may find the new dates advantageous. This situation underscores the need for flexibility and adaptability in family planning, particularly in the context of the UAE's dynamic educational calendar. 

For parents choosing schools in Dubai, understanding the implications of such changes is crucial for future holiday planning and overall family logistics. The unified academic calendar serves to streamline the educational experience, but it also requires families to stay vigilant about any adjustments that may arise.
