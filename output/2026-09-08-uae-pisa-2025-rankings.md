## Significant Gains in PISA 2025 Rankings

The UAE has achieved remarkable success in the Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) 2025, emerging as the top-performing Arab nation in science, mathematics, and reading. This achievement is particularly noteworthy as it comes at a time when many countries are experiencing declines in educational performance. The results, which include contributions from over 34,600 students across 1,070 [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), demonstrate both an overall improvement in scores and a notable reduction in low-performing students.

## Details of the Performance Improvements

In the latest PISA assessment, UAE students recorded significant advancements, with scores rising in all three subjects. Science scores increased by 26 points to 458, mathematics saw a 21-point rise to 453, and reading improved by 15 points to 433. This comprehensive progress places the UAE among a select group of eight countries out of 91 participants that improved across all three domains since the last assessment in 2022.

The educational performance of students in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) was particularly impressive, with scores significantly exceeding the UAE averages. In science, Dubai students scored 506, surpassing the UAE average by 48 points. In mathematics, the score was 496, which is 43 points higher than the national average, while in reading, students achieved a score of 484, exceeding the UAE average by 51 points. This performance also placed Dubai students above the OECD average in all three subjects, with notable margins.

## Factors Contributing to Success

The UAE's educational progress can be attributed to several key factors. There has been a concerted effort to enhance the quality of teaching and learning, including ongoing curriculum development and professional development for educators. The integration of technology into the classroom has also played a crucial role, with 81.7% of students reporting they learned to evaluate AI-generated information in class, compared to just 62.6% of students in OECD countries.

Moreover, a significant majority of UAE students are learning in environments where subject-specific guidelines for digital device use are established, with 96.3% attending schools that have such protocols in place. This commitment to integrating digital literacy into the curriculum aligns with the UAE's broader ambitions towards fostering advanced skills in preparation for future challenges.

## Implications for Parents and Students

The results of the PISA 2025 assessment are a positive indicator for parents considering educational options for their children in the UAE. The continuous improvements in educational outcomes suggest that schools are effectively preparing students for real-world challenges. The emphasis on critical thinking, digital literacy, and responsible technology use is particularly relevant in today’s rapidly changing job market.

Such collaboration is essential in maintaining momentum and ensuring that educational practices remain aligned with international best practices.
