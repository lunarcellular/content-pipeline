## UAE Achieves Top Ranking in PISA 2025

The UAE has achieved a remarkable feat by ranking first in the Arab world in the PISA 2025 international student assessment, demonstrating significant improvement across key performance areas. This assessment, overseen by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), involved 34,639 students from 1,070 [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the country.

## Significant Improvements in Student Performance

The latest results indicate an overall average improvement of 26 points for UAE students, with notable enhancements of 21 points in mathematics and 15 points in reading. This marks a substantial achievement, placing the UAE among just eight countries worldwide that improved their performance across all three assessment areas—mathematics, reading, and science—out of 91 participating countries.

The Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) evaluates the ability of 15-year-old students to apply their knowledge and skills to real-world challenges, providing crucial insights into the effectiveness of education systems globally. The increased scores reflect not only enhanced academic performance but also a growing focus on equipping students with essential skills for the future.

## Emphasis on Digital Literacy and AI Skills

The PISA 2025 results highlight the UAE's commitment to preparing students for a rapidly evolving technological landscape. A substantial 96.3% of school pupils in the UAE receive specific guidance on the use of digital devices, while 81.7% are taught how to critically evaluate information generated by artificial intelligence. This integration of digital literacy and AI-related skills into the curriculum underscores the country's proactive approach to education in the digital age.

The recognition of these efforts not only enhances the UAE's standing in international education but also sets a benchmark for other countries in the region. The emphasis on future skills is expected to drive further improvements in the quality of education, aligning with the UAE's broader goals of economic diversification and technological advancement.
