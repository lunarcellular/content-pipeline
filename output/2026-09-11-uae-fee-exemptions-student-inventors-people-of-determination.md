## New Fee Exemptions Effective September 14, 2026

The UAE has announced new fee exemptions for student inventors and People of Determination, aimed at reducing costs associated with key intellectual property (IP) services. Under a Cabinet resolution, which comes into effect on September 14, 2026, these groups will benefit from exemptions on specific government fees related to patents and industrial designs.

## Details of the Exemptions

The resolution, released as Cabinet Resolution No. 136 of 2026, outlines that both student inventors and People of Determination will be exempt from fees for designated services within the existing schedule provided by the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. It was published in the Official Gazette on August 14, 2026, and is part of a broader initiative to encourage innovation and support individuals with disabilities.

The exemptions apply specifically to a range of services, including:

- Filing applications for patents and industrial designs
- Procedures for ownership changes and amendments
- Appeals or grievances
- Certified copies and official certificates
- International patent applications

For People of Determination, additional fee exemptions are available for services that focus on substantive examination and expedited review procedures. Similarly, student inventors will receive further exemptions for specific technical examination services critical to the patent application process.

## Financial Implications

The new policy does not eliminate all fees associated with patent applications but reduces the burden significantly for eligible applicants. Under the current fee structure, patent examination fees can be substantial, with estimates ranging from Dh7,000 to Dh9,000 depending on the complexity of the application. By exempting certain fees, the UAE aims to alleviate financial constraints for aspiring inventors and individuals with special needs, fostering a more inclusive environment for innovation.

## Context and Importance

This initiative is part of the UAE's ongoing commitment to support innovation and entrepreneurship, particularly among youth and underrepresented groups. The industrial property rights framework, guided by Federal Law No. 11 of 2021, aims to protect inventions and promote a culture of creativity. By facilitating access to essential services, the government is not only encouraging individual growth but also enhancing the overall innovation landscape in the UAE.

The new fee exemptions are expected to make a significant impact, particularly as they help reduce the financial barriers faced by emerging inventors and those with disabilities in navigating the complexities of IP rights. This aligns with the UAE's vision of becoming a hub for creativity and innovation, further empowering individuals to contribute to the economy.
