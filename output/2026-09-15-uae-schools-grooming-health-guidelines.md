As the 2026-27 academic year commences, [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the UAE are implementing stricter guidelines regarding student grooming, uniform standards, and the consumption of energy drinks. These changes are aimed at fostering a focused learning environment while promoting the health and wellbeing of students.

## New Guidelines on Grooming and Uniforms

Schools are increasingly tightening their policies on grooming, which includes restrictions on extreme hairstyles. This encompasses prohibiting styles where parts of the head are shaved while other areas remain long, as well as restrictions on long hair for male students. Additionally, many institutions have banned the wearing of hoodies within school premises, seeking to standardise uniform policies and enhance the overall learning atmosphere.

In communication with parents, numerous schools have explicitly detailed new restrictions on food and beverages that students are allowed to bring onto campus. This shift is part of a broader effort to ensure that students maintain a healthy diet while at school.

## Focus on Health and Wellbeing

One prominent aspect of these revised guidelines involves a concerted effort to limit the consumption of energy drinks on school grounds. Several schools have indicated that these drinks pose potential health risks for children and adolescents, aligning with existing health policies advocated by regulatory authorities. For instance, the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) has been proactive in promoting healthy food and nutrition policies, which explicitly prohibit energy drinks on campuses.

The rationale behind these restrictions is largely preventative. School leaders are keen on creating a disciplined environment that supports not just academic success but also the physical health of their students. By establishing clear guidelines around grooming and diet, schools aim to instil a sense of responsibility and respect among students.

## Communication and Enforcement of Policies

The implementation of these guidelines varies from school to school, with some adopting more specific rules than others. Many institutions have adopted a collaborative approach to enforce these policies. Staff members are encouraged to have constructive conversations with students regarding compliance with grooming standards. When expectations are not met, schools typically involve parents in discussions to ensure alignment and support for these policies.

The emphasis on maintaining high standards of grooming and dietary habits reflects a growing recognition of the role that a supportive school environment plays in student development. Leaders across various schools have expressed that these measures are not intended as punitive. Instead, they are designed to create an inclusive atmosphere where every student can thrive academically and socially.

## Implications for Parents and Students

For parents in the UAE, these changes signal an evolving educational landscape that prioritises both academic performance and personal health. As schools enforce stricter guidelines regarding appearance and dietary choices, parents may need to adapt their support strategies in line with these policies. 

The introduction of these grooming and health guidelines may also influence parents’ decisions when selecting educational institutions for their children. Understanding a school's commitment to fostering a disciplined and healthy environment could play a pivotal role in the decision-making process.

In summary, the tightening of grooming and health policies across UAE schools represents a strategic initiative to enhance student wellbeing and academic focus. As educational institutions continue to navigate these changes, parents are encouraged to engage with schools to better understand how these guidelines will impact their children’s educational experience.
