## Abu Dhabi Expands AI Education Initiative

[Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi)'s Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) is launching a significant initiative to integrate artificial intelligence (AI) education across more than 170 private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in the emirate. The programme aims to equip students with essential AI skills from kindergarten through Grade 12, emphasising their health and wellbeing throughout the educational experience.

### A Comprehensive Framework for AI Skills Development

The AI Education Initiative provides an integrated framework that allows students to progressively build their understanding of AI technologies. Starting in the early years, the focus is on developing critical and logical thinking through engaging, age-appropriate activities that minimise screen time. As students advance through the grades, they will learn to utilise AI tools, critically evaluate their outputs, and apply them to real-world problems.

The approach ensures that AI serves as an assistive tool, enhancing students’ capabilities rather than replacing independent thought. By the time students reach Grade 12, they will be equipped to design scalable AI-based systems, transitioning from technology users to innovators.

### Learning Outcomes and Ethical Considerations

ADEK defines clear learning outcomes that include:

- **Critical Engagement**: Students will learn to critically evaluate and interact with AI technologies.
- **System Design**: They will develop the skills necessary to design and build AI solutions.
- **Responsible Use**: The curriculum will address governance, data protection, privacy, and the societal impacts of AI.

This framework is informed by collaboration with experts from esteemed international institutions, ensuring alignment with global educational standards.

### A Progressive Learning Pathway

The AI curriculum is structured to provide a progressive learning pathway that evolves with students' capabilities. It begins with foundational stages, such as "Explorer" and "Emerging Thinker", and progresses to advanced levels, culminating in "Systems and Applications Designer" by Grade 12. This structured approach allows for a comprehensive educational experience that grows with the student.

Schools have the flexibility to incorporate the AI curriculum into existing subjects, deliver it through a hybrid model, or offer it as a standalone subject, ensuring that the specified learning outcomes are met effectively.

### Assessment and Monitoring of AI Skills

To track students' progress and proficiency in AI, ADEK has introduced the AI Skills Growth Assessment. This standardised assessment is administered biannually to students from Grades 4 to 12, allowing schools to identify individual learning needs and monitor development over time. Students requiring additional support will receive targeted lessons to help them master foundational skills essential for progression.

### Supporting the Technology and AI Policy

The initiative is part of ADEK's broader Technology and AI Policy for Schools, which outlines the requirements for the use of AI technologies in private schools. This policy mandates that schools implement AI-powered educational tools that align with ADEK's standards, ensuring a responsible and enriching learning environment.

The policy also includes safeguards to maintain human oversight of AI-generated decisions and measures to ensure content safety and alignment with the UAE's values. These provisions are designed to enhance the educational experience while fostering critical thinking and problem-solving abilities among students.

### Implications for Parents in Abu Dhabi

The expansion of AI education in Abu Dhabi represents a substantial advancement in the emirate's educational landscape. For parents, this initiative offers reassurance that their children will gain critical skills necessary for the future job market, particularly in technology-driven fields. The emphasis on ethical AI use and critical thinking prepares students not only to navigate existing technologies but also to contribute to the development of future innovations.

As the initiative unfolds, parents can expect their children to engage in a learning environment that prioritises both academic achievement and personal development, equipping them with the tools needed to thrive in an increasingly digital world. This initiative positions Abu Dhabi as a forward-thinking leader in educational innovation, addressing the demands of a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
