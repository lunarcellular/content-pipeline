## Abu Dhabi's Commitment to Early Childhood Education

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) is set to significantly expand early childhood education in the emirate by adding 12,000 [nursery](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries) seats by 2029. This initiative aims to enhance access to high-quality, subsidised early education for Emirati children aged two to four, thereby promoting a strong foundation in language, culture, and national identity.

## Phased Expansion for Greater Accessibility

The expansion will be rolled out in three phases, with a focus on areas that have a high concentration of Emirati children. This strategic approach ensures that families in these regions can benefit from the new nurseries, which are designed to provide an enriching early learning environment. Existing facilities of Al Ghad Nurseries will be complemented by new locations across Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, and Al Dhafra.

## Enrolment and Fees

Families will have the opportunity to enrol their children in these nurseries for a nominal fee of Dh800 per month. Notably, there will be exemptions for low-income families registered with the Social Support Authority, children requiring additional support, and the fifth child in families with five or more children. This fee structure is designed to alleviate financial barriers for eligible families, ensuring that a wider demographic can access quality early education.

## Developmental Focus

The nurseries will not only serve as educational institutions but also aim to foster children's language, communication, social, and learning skills. Additionally, they will play a crucial role in identifying any potential developmental or learning needs early on, allowing for tailored support to be provided as necessary.

## Career Opportunities for Emirati Women

The expansion is also set to create significant employment opportunities within the early childhood sector, particularly for Emirati women. ADEK anticipates that the growth of the nursery network will lead to the creation of over 1,000 new jobs. This initiative aligns with broader governmental goals to empower Emirati women and enhance their participation in the workforce, especially in fields that contribute to the educational landscape.

## Conclusion

The planned expansion of nursery seats in Abu Dhabi represents a proactive step towards enhancing early childhood education access for Emirati families. By prioritising quality education and cultural values, this initiative not only benefits children but also supports the development of the local workforce. As such, it is a noteworthy development for parents considering early education options in the emirate.
