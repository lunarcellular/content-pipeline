## Abu Dhabi's New Initiative for Arabic Reading Skills

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) is set to launch a comprehensive teacher-training programme aimed at enhancing Arabic reading skills among kindergarten students, beginning next year. This initiative arises in response to a growing concern regarding children's declining proficiency in Arabic, particularly in reading.

### Addressing the Challenge

The challenges surrounding Arabic literacy have become increasingly evident, with indicators suggesting that children are engaging less with the language and struggling to achieve reading levels comparable to those seen a decade ago. This trend appears to emerge as early as age five, prompting ADEK's proactive approach to address the issue.

Mariam Al Halami, director of the private education sector at ADEK, highlighted the significance of this training programme, noting that the effectiveness of teaching methodologies has not kept pace with the evolving educational landscape. Many educators currently lack a structured approach to teaching reading.

### Programme Structure and Methodology

The new training initiative will be grounded in the "Science of Reading" methodology, which is based on over two decades of research into effective reading instruction. The programme will focus on foundational skills, beginning with teaching letter sounds, progressing to blending these sounds into words, and ultimately advancing to comprehension skills.

This structured approach will not be limited to kindergarten; it will also cater to students in Grade 1 and Grade 2 who may require additional support in reading. Teachers will employ a straightforward assessment tool that enables them to gauge each child's reading progress and tailor their instruction accordingly.

### Intensive Training for Educators

Teachers participating in this programme will undergo intensive, certified training to equip them with the necessary tools and methodologies to assist struggling readers effectively. This emphasis on professional development is crucial, as it ensures that educators are well-prepared to implement the new strategies in their classrooms.

### The Role of Digital Learning

In addition to traditional teaching methods, Al Halami underscored the potential of digital games to enhance Arabic language learning and promote cultural identity. She noted that while some local companies have started to integrate national identity into their educational games, there remains a significant gap between the gaming industry and educational institutions. Bridging this divide could facilitate a broader and more impactful educational experience for children.

### Implications for Parents and Schools

For parents considering their children's education in Abu Dhabi, this initiative signals a commitment to improving Arabic literacy at an early age. As [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) prepare to implement this new training programme, parents can expect a more structured approach to language education that is responsive to the challenges faced by young learners.

The focus on professional development for teachers also indicates a long-term investment in educational quality. Parents may find reassurance in the knowledge that their children will be taught by educators equipped with effective methodologies to foster language skills.

As Abu Dhabi continues to enhance its educational framework, the introduction of this Arabic reading training programme marks a significant step towards nurturing a new generation of proficient Arabic speakers, ensuring that cultural and linguistic heritage is preserved and celebrated.

This initiative also reflects a broader trend in educational reform within the UAE, as authorities strive to respond to the evolving needs of students and the demands of a globalised world. Parents are encouraged to stay informed about these developments, as they can greatly impact their children's learning experiences and outcomes in the coming years.
