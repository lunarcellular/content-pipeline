## Dubai Schools Expand to Meet Rising Demand

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private education sector is set to add approximately 18,000 affordable seats over the next two academic years, responding to a 4.6% increase in student enrolment. This surge brings the total number of students in private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) to 406,563, up from around 388,731 just a year earlier. The growth in enrolment reflects Dubai's ongoing commitment to providing accessible education options for families.

### New Institutions and Early Childhood Centres

The expansion in student capacity is facilitated by the opening of 26 new private educational institutions this academic year, which includes 17 early childhood centres and seven schools. These new establishments are expected to increase the number of available seats significantly, with nearly 17,000 seats from schools and over 1,700 from early childhood centres. This increase is part of a broader strategy to accommodate the sustained demand for private education in the emirate.

### Strategic Educational Initiatives

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has outlined its strategic priorities for the 2026-27 academic year, which include expanding affordable educational options, enhancing the performance of schools with high concentrations of Emirati students, and strengthening cultural identity and Arabic language education. Other key areas of focus are student wellbeing, parental empowerment, and the development of Emirati talent within the education ecosystem.

The KHDA also highlighted the successful implementation of the Dubai Education 33 (E33) Strategy, which aims to transform the educational landscape by 2033. Since the launch of this strategy, more than 20 initiatives and 28 regulatory frameworks have been developed to enhance the quality and accessibility of education.

### Performance Improvements and Future Prospects

Dubai's private schools have shown significant improvements in performance, with Emirati students achieving notable gains in mathematics, science, and reading. The KHDA reported that the performance of Emirati students improved by 31 points in mathematics, 30 points in science, and 18 points in reading compared to previous assessments. This progress indicates that the educational strategies being implemented are beginning to yield positive results.

As quality assurance visits to private schools resume, the KHDA is committed to maintaining high-performance standards through a transparent and supportive regulatory approach. This commitment to quality assurance is crucial as the education sector continues to grow and evolve.

### Councils for Stakeholder Engagement

The upcoming academic year will also see the establishment of several councils aimed at enhancing stakeholder engagement within the education sector. The Dubai Students Council, Dubai Parents Council, and Dubai Educators Council will provide platforms for students, parents, and educators to contribute to the development of educational policies and practices. This initiative aligns with the KHDA's goal of empowering families and fostering a collaborative educational environment.

### Implications for Parents

For parents navigating the educational landscape in Dubai, the addition of affordable seats and new institutions presents a significant opportunity. The expansion of options allows families greater flexibility in choosing educational pathways that best suit their children's needs. As Dubai continues to enhance its educational offerings, parents can look forward to a more diverse and competitive schooling environment, ultimately benefiting their children's educational experiences and outcomes.

In summary, the proactive measures being taken to expand private school capacity and improve educational quality demonstrate Dubai's commitment to fostering an inclusive and high-quality education system that meets the demands of its growing population.
