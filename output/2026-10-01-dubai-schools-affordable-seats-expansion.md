## Enrolment Growth and Expansion Plans

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private education sector is set to expand significantly over the next two academic years, with plans to add approximately 18,000 affordable seats in response to a growing student population. As of the new academic year, enrolment in private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) reached around 406,563, reflecting a 4.6% increase from the previous year’s figures. The rise in student numbers indicates a sustained demand for private education options in the emirate.

The expansion comes as part of broader efforts by education authorities to widen access to quality educational institutions. A total of 26 new private educational institutions have opened this academic year, comprising 17 early childhood centres and nine schools, which contributes nearly 17,000 new seats to the private education landscape.

## Priorities of Education Authorities

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has outlined several key priorities for the 2026-27 academic year. These include increasing access to affordable education, improving school performance, particularly in schools with high concentrations of Emirati students, and enhancing student wellbeing and cultural identity. The KHDA is also focused on the empowerment of parents and the development of Emirati talent within the education sector.

The ongoing implementation of Dubai's Education 33 (E33) Strategy aims to transform the educational ecosystem by introducing innovative initiatives and policies. Since the strategy's launch, more than 20 initiatives and 28 policies have been developed, emphasising quality assurance, digital transformation, and inclusivity within the education system.

## Impact on Families and Educational Choices

For families in Dubai, the growth in the number of affordable school seats and the introduction of new institutions represent a significant opportunity to access a wider range of educational options. The increasing demand for private education is reflected not only in student enrolment figures but also in the variety of curricula available, catering to diverse preferences and educational philosophies.

The establishment of two globally recognised universities in Dubai further enhances the emirate's position as a global education hub. These institutions contribute to the higher education landscape, providing families with more choices for advanced studies and professional development.

## Future Developments in Education

KHDA’s commitment to ongoing quality assurance measures ensures that educational institutions will maintain high-performance standards as they grow. The return of quality assurance visits to private schools this academic year is expected to reinforce these standards and support schools in their development efforts.

Additionally, the formation of councils for students, parents, and educators in Dubai will provide essential platforms for community engagement and feedback. These councils aim to facilitate communication between stakeholders and contribute to shaping the future of education in the emirate.

In summary, as Dubai schools expand their capacity to accommodate rising student numbers and enhance educational offerings, parents can look forward to a more diverse and accessible education landscape. This ongoing evolution underscores Dubai's commitment to fostering a high-quality education system that meets the needs of its growing population.
