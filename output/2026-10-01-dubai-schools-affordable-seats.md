## Affordable Seats in Dubai Schools: Major Expansion Ahead

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are set to expand significantly, adding approximately 18,000 affordable seats over the next two academic years. This growth is in response to a notable rise in enrolment, with the emirate's private schools experiencing a 4.6 per cent increase, now hosting 406,563 students compared to around 388,731 in June 2026.

The expansion of the private education sector is marked by the opening of 26 new educational institutions this academic year, which includes seven schools, 17 early childhood centres, and two international higher education establishments. The seven new schools alone have contributed nearly 17,000 additional seats, while the early childhood centres are expected to provide over 1,700 places. 

## Strategic Education Initiatives

This expansion aligns with Dubai's long-term educational ambitions, as outlined in the Education 33 Strategy, which aims to enhance the overall educational ecosystem in the emirate by 2033. The emphasis is on creating a responsive education system that meets the evolving needs of students and bolsters Dubai's global competitiveness.

Over the past two academic years, the introduction of 51 new private educational institutions has further increased capacity across early childhood, school, and higher education sectors. This growth is complemented by strong performance metrics, with Dubai's private schools ranking among the top ten education systems globally across all PISA 2025 assessment domains.

## Focus on Emirati Student Outcomes

Improvements in student outcomes are evident, especially among Emirati students, whose scores in mathematics, science, and reading have seen significant increases. For instance, their scores rose by 31 points in mathematics, 30 points in science, and 18 points in reading, indicating a positive trend in educational achievement.

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has also identified various priorities for the 2026-27 academic year, including enhancing the performance of schools with high concentrations of Emirati students, promoting cultural identity and Arabic language education, and improving student wellbeing and protection.

## Engaging Parents and Educators

The new academic year will introduce the second Dubai Students Council, alongside the launch of the first Dubai Parents Council and Dubai Educators Council. These councils are designed to provide a direct platform for students, parents, and educators to contribute to the development of the education sector, ensuring the learner journey is shaped by all stakeholders.

In an effort to enhance parent engagement, KHDA has organised over 20 awareness sessions since the adoption of the Education 33 Strategy, reaching more than 5,000 parents. Topics covered include positive parenting, family resilience, digital safety, and strategies for supporting children during stressful periods.

## Continued Expansion of Higher Education

Beyond primary and secondary education, Dubai is also expanding its higher education landscape. In the last three years, eleven new higher education institutions have been established, alongside a 29 per cent increase in enrolment numbers. Prominent global institutions are also expanding their presence, with the American University of Beirut's Dubai campus opening in September 2026 and Peking University HSBC Business School preparing to launch MBA and EMBA programmes in January 2027.

The overall growth of Dubai's private education sector, including early childhood education, schools, and higher education, has maintained an annual growth rate of 4.6 per cent over the past three years. This trend reflects the emirate's commitment to fostering a robust educational environment that meets the diverse needs of its population.

## Conclusion

The anticipated addition of affordable seats in Dubai schools is a response to the rising demand for quality education in the emirate. As families increasingly choose Dubai as their home, the strategic initiatives under the Education 33 Strategy aim to create a more inclusive and effective educational ecosystem. For parents, this means greater access to educational opportunities and a commitment to enhancing student outcomes, ultimately contributing to a thriving community of learners.
