## KHDA Approves Flexible School Timings in Dubai

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has announced that [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) can now change their timings to alleviate traffic congestion and improve the daily commute for students and teachers. This decision aims to promote student well-being while ensuring that parents' feedback is taken into account. However, any proposed changes to school timings must receive prior approval from KHDA.

The shift towards flexible school timings comes as part of the broader Dubai Education Masterplan, which seeks to encourage more students to travel to school using sustainable modes of transport, such as walking, cycling, or using scooters. The initiative reflects a commitment to creating a more accessible urban environment where all essential services are within a 20-minute reach.

### Understanding the Approval Process

KHDA has made it clear that it cannot impose uniform school timings across Dubai due to the diversity of curriculums and extracurricular activities offered by different schools. Each school's unique needs will influence its operational hours. Schools wishing to adjust their schedules must adhere to specific conditions prioritising the welfare of students and considering parental opinions.

This approach has been shaped by past experiences where changes in school timings led to parental complaints. KHDA will closely monitor such adjustments to ensure they meet the established criteria and benefit the school community.

### The Vision for Sustainable Commuting

The initiative is also aligned with Dubai's urban planning goals, which aim to create a city where residents can access amenities within 20 minutes. KHDA emphasises the importance of fostering a culture of active commuting among students, encouraging methods like walking and cycling over reliance on vehicles or electric scooters. This vision promotes not only a healthier lifestyle but also contributes to reducing traffic congestion in the city.

The Director General of KHDA has highlighted the importance of safeguarding children's well-being and academic success as core priorities in any changes made. The authority is currently exploring innovative educational models, including hybrid learning systems that blend physical and remote education, though no formal approval for such models has been granted yet.

### Implications for Parents and Students

For parents, this new flexibility in school timings may provide opportunities for improved scheduling, allowing families to better manage their daily routines. Additionally, the focus on sustainable commuting aligns with growing concerns over traffic congestion and environmental impacts, making it an appealing option for many families.

However, parents are encouraged to actively participate in discussions regarding any changes to ensure that their children's needs are adequately addressed. KHDA's willingness to consider parental feedback is a positive step towards creating a more collaborative educational environment.

In conclusion, the KHDA's initiative to allow flexible school timings represents a significant development in Dubai's education landscape, aiming to enhance student well-being while addressing practical issues such as traffic congestion. As schools begin to explore these opportunities, parents should stay informed and engaged in the process to ensure their voices are heard.
