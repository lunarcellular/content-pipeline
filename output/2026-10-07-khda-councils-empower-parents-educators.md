## KHDA Initiatives to Strengthen Community Engagement in Dubai Schools

In a significant move to enhance collaboration within the educational community, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has launched three new councils aimed at empowering parents, educators, and students in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools). These initiatives are part of Dubai's Education 33 (E33) Strategy, which focuses on fostering partnerships that enrich the educational experience.

### Formation of New Councils

The newly established councils include the Dubai Parents Council, the Dubai Educators Council, and the second cohort of the Dubai Students Council. Each of these councils comprises 15 members selected from a wide pool of nominations. The Dubai Parents Council will feature representatives from both Emirati and expatriate families, while the Dubai Educators Council will include teachers and education professionals. The Dubai Students Council will consist of 16 students from Grades 9 to 12, allowing them to actively participate in shaping education policies.

KHDA reported receiving substantial interest in these councils, with 131 nominations for the Students Council, 152 for the Parents Council, and 160 for the Educators Council. The selection process was designed to be transparent and fair, ensuring a diverse representation of the community.

### Objectives and Expected Outcomes

The councils aim to create platforms for meaningful dialogue between stakeholders in education, allowing them to share insights and perspectives. The Dubai Parents Council will work on strengthening school-family partnerships and addressing common priorities, while the Educators Council will provide an advisory role, sharing evidence-based insights from classroom experiences. The councils are expected to contribute to policy development and initiatives that enhance teacher wellbeing and improve student learning experiences.

This initiative reflects a broader commitment to making education in Dubai more responsive to the needs of families and educators. By integrating the voices of parents, educators, and students, KHDA aims to build a more inclusive and effective education system.

### Impact on Parents and Educational Choices

For parents considering schools in Dubai, the establishment of these councils represents a positive step towards greater involvement in their children's education. The councils are designed to ensure that the concerns and aspirations of families are heard and taken into account in educational policy decisions.

The focus on collaboration and community input may also lead to improvements in educational standards and student outcomes, making schools in Dubai more attuned to the diverse needs of their populations. Parents can expect to see greater transparency and opportunities for engagement in the educational process.

As the councils begin their work, they are poised to play a crucial role in shaping the educational landscape in Dubai, fostering a culture of partnership that benefits students, parents, and educators alike.
