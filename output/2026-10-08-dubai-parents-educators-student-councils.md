## Dubai Education Councils: Enhancing Engagement in Schools

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) has introduced its first Parents Council and Educators Council, alongside the renewal of the Students Council for the 2026-27 academic year. This initiative, spearheaded by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), aims to foster collaboration among parents, educators, and students, aligning with the emirate's Education 33 (E33) Strategy.

### Formation of the Councils

The newly established councils will consist of 15 parents, 15 educators, and 16 students from Grades 9 to 12 representing various private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in Dubai. The formation reflects a significant step towards enhancing the involvement of families and educational professionals in shaping the education system.

KHDA received a considerable number of nominations: 131 for the Students Council, 152 for the Parents Council, and 160 for the Educators Council. The selection process prioritised fairness, transparency, and diversity, ensuring representation from Emirati educators among the council members.

### Objectives of the Councils

The primary goal of these councils is to create a structured platform for dialogue and collaboration. The Students Council will serve as a direct channel for students to voice their experiences and ideas regarding their learning environments. This engagement is expected to provide valuable insights into education policy and student experiences.

The Parents Council aims to strengthen partnerships between schools and families, addressing shared priorities that affect students' learning and overall wellbeing. This council will empower parents to engage directly with KHDA, moving beyond the limitations of individual school feedback mechanisms.

The Educators Council will incorporate insights from teachers and education professionals, allowing for a more comprehensive approach to educational policy. This body is expected to provide evidence-based input on various initiatives, including those focused on inclusion and improving the educational experiences for students.

### Significance for the Education Community

The establishment of these councils aligns with KHDA's vision of creating a learner-centred and future-ready education system. By promoting active participation from all stakeholders—students, parents, and educators—the councils are designed to address the evolving needs of the Dubai education landscape.

The councils will operate on a voluntary, unpaid basis for a term of one year, with members eligible to apply for a second term based on their performance. This structure not only encourages committed participation but also fosters a sense of community among diverse educational stakeholders.

### Conclusion

The launch of the Parents, Educators, and Students Councils marks a pivotal development in Dubai's educational framework, reinforcing the idea that education is a shared responsibility. By integrating the voices of various stakeholders, KHDA is taking significant steps to enhance the educational experience for all involved. For parents, this initiative offers a formalised avenue to contribute to the conversation surrounding their children's education, potentially leading to more responsive and inclusive educational policies.
