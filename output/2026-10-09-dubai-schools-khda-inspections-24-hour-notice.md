## New KHDA Inspection Methodology for Dubai Schools

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) will encounter a significant shift in the approach to quality assurance assessments starting from the first term of the 2026-27 academic year. The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) will implement a revised inspection methodology that includes a 24-hour notice period before evaluations take place. This new strategy aims to enhance the quality of education and improve the overall experience for students.

### AI-Driven Assessments Tailored to School Needs

The KHDA's approach will leverage artificial intelligence (AI) and performance data to create flexible assessments tailored to the unique needs and development stages of each school. This data-driven strategy is designed to provide a more accurate representation of students' daily experiences while allowing schools to identify specific areas for improvement effectively.

Two types of quality assurance visits will be introduced: comprehensive assessments and shorter, targeted follow-up visits. New schools in their first or second years will undergo specialised assessments focusing on teaching quality, student experiences, and the support needed for improvement.

### Comprehensive and Targeted Quality Assurance Visits

Schools entering their third year of operation will receive comprehensive evaluations examining overall performance, teaching standards, and learning outcomes. These assessments will conclude with a detailed report and an overall rating for the school. In contrast, other eligible schools may receive either a comprehensive or a shorter follow-up assessment, depending on their performance data and identified needs.

The targeted assessments will concentrate on specific areas based on school data and performance indicators, offering feedback on strengths and areas requiring improvement. However, these shorter visits will not result in a new overall school rating.

### Focus Areas for the Upcoming Assessments

This academic year's evaluations will prioritise early childhood education and the quality of education provided to Emirati students, alongside inclusive education and the academic progress of students with special needs. Particular attention will be given to the teaching and learning of Arabic, assessing the effectiveness of different learning approaches on students' skills in reading, writing, comprehension, and communication.

The new inspection framework aligns with Dubai's Education 33 Strategy, which focuses on improving educational outcomes and developing Emirati talent. The KHDA’s revised approach aims to place students at the heart of school assessments, ensuring that educational quality is consistent and accessible for all.

### Implications for Parents

This new inspection process is crucial for parents in Dubai, as it directly impacts the educational quality their children receive. With 81 percent of students in private schools rated as receiving good or better quality education, the updates to the KHDA's assessment approach may further enhance transparency and accountability in schools. The emphasis on tailored evaluations means that schools will have a better opportunity to address the unique challenges they face, thereby improving the overall educational landscape.

Parents can expect that the new inspection methodology will lead to more informed choices when selecting schools for their children, as schools will be evaluated more frequently and with greater focus on individual student outcomes. This development not only benefits schools but also ensures that parents have confidence in the quality of education being offered in Dubai.

In summary, the KHDA's 24-hour notice for inspections marks a pivotal change in how educational quality is assessed in Dubai, promising a more nuanced and responsive approach to school evaluations that prioritises student experiences and educational outcomes.
