## New Inspection Model for Dubai Schools

As [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) prepare for the 2026-27 academic year, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) is reintroducing school inspections after a two-year hiatus. This significant change raises questions about how a school's inspection rating may impact its ability to increase fees, a concern for many parents in the region.

KHDA's new framework includes two types of inspection visits: Quality Assurance 1 (QA 1) and Quality Assurance 2 (QA 2). The QA 1 inspection is a comprehensive assessment of the entire school based on the UAE School Inspection Framework established in 2015, which evaluates key areas such as student achievement, teaching quality, and leadership standards. Conversely, QA 2 visits are targeted inspections focused on specific areas identified for improvement without altering the school's overall rating.

## Implications of Inspection Ratings on Fees

The results of these inspections will directly influence a school’s eligibility to raise fees. If a fee increase is approved, it will depend on the school’s most recent inspection rating. For schools undergoing QA 1 inspections, the new rating—whether it improves, declines, or remains the same—will determine their eligibility for fee adjustments. In contrast, schools receiving QA 2 inspections will be assessed based on their previous ratings, since these targeted visits do not affect the existing overall rating.

For instance, if a school is rated "Very Good" and undergoes a QA 2 inspection, it will maintain that rating. Therefore, if a fee increase is sought, it will be evaluated against the previous rating, which may limit the school's ability to raise fees.

## Timing and Frequency of Inspections

KHDA's inspections will occur with little notice, providing schools with no more than 24 hours' advance warning. This strategy aims to ensure that schools maintain high standards throughout the academic year rather than preparing specifically for an inspection date. The Director-General of KHDA, emphasised that this approach allows inspectors to observe genuine classroom interactions and learning environments, which is critical for accurate assessments.

Furthermore, schools entering their third year of operation will automatically undergo a QA 1 inspection, which is essential for establishing a baseline for their performance. This structured approach is designed to utilise KHDA's resources effectively and focus on schools that may need additional support.

## Priorities for School Inspections

KHDA has outlined four key areas of focus for its inspections: early childhood education, quality education for Emirati students, inclusive education for students of determination, and the teaching and learning of the Arabic language. These priorities highlight the authority's commitment to fostering a high-quality educational landscape that meets diverse student needs.

The updated inspection model aims to provide more relevant data to parents regarding school performance and fee structures. Parents will be able to understand how a school's inspection rating can influence tuition costs, thereby making informed decisions about their children's education.

## Conclusion: What This Means for Parents

The reintroduction of inspections and the new framework for assessing schools in Dubai marks a pivotal moment for parents navigating the complex landscape of private education. The connection between inspection ratings and fee increases will undoubtedly shape decisions as parents seek the best educational opportunities for their children.

As schools prepare for these inspections, parents should stay informed about their child's school's performance ratings and how they might affect tuition costs in the coming academic year. Understanding this dynamic will be crucial for families as they plan their educational expenses and choose the right school for their children in Dubai.
