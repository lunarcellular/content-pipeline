## Resumption of Inspections

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are preparing for a significant shift as school inspections resume after a two-year hiatus. The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has announced that inspectors may arrive with as little as 24 hours' notice, requiring schools to maintain consistent teaching standards and operational practices throughout the academic year.

This unprecedented change aims to provide a more accurate assessment of the everyday educational environment, moving away from the previous model where schools received approximately a week's notice before inspections. The intent is to ensure that inspections reflect actual daily practices rather than a curated version prepared specifically for evaluation.

## Implications for School Leadership

The new inspection model presents a challenge for school leaders who must ensure that teaching standards, assessment records, and classroom practices are consistently maintained. Many principals have begun to rely on regular classroom observations, internal reviews, and ongoing self-evaluation to keep their schools inspection-ready. This shift acknowledges the professional pressure that assessments can create while promoting a culture of continuous improvement.

KHDA's new framework also includes collecting updated data from schools in October. This data will cover key areas such as student attainment, leadership effectiveness, staff training, and overall teaching and learning quality. Quality assurance visits will commence once the authority has compiled and reviewed this data, although a specific start date for inspections has not yet been provided.

## A New Approach to Self-Evaluation

One of the significant changes introduced is the treatment of the School Self-Evaluation Form (SEF). Schools are now encouraged to view this document as a continuously updated tool rather than a one-off submission. This approach aligns more closely with ongoing school improvement processes, fostering a culture of reflection and development throughout the year.

The emphasis on maintaining high standards daily rather than only during inspection periods has been positively received by many in the education sector. The new model encourages a deeper engagement with the quality of education being provided, rather than focusing solely on preparation for inspections.

## Strategies for Continuous Improvement

Several schools have already implemented internal monitoring systems to ensure quality teaching and learning. These systems include structured classroom visits, known as "learning walks," which allow school leaders to observe teaching practices and student engagement in real time. Such proactive measures help leaders respond quickly to areas needing improvement, embedding quality assurance into the daily operations of the school.

This year-round readiness model is aimed at reducing disruption to students when an inspection is announced. Schools are expected to maintain assessment data, curriculum plans, and protocols continuously, ensuring that when an inspection notification arrives, there will be no need to pivot or alter normal routines drastically.

## Conclusion: What This Means for Parents

The return of school inspections in Dubai, with the introduction of a 24-hour notice period, marks a significant development in the education landscape. For parents, this change may provide a clearer and more authentic picture of how schools operate on a daily basis. The focus on consistent quality rather than inspection preparation could lead to better educational outcomes for students.

As schools adapt to this new approach, parents can expect to see a more transparent evaluation process that reflects the true performance of educational institutions. This shift underscores the importance of maintaining high standards throughout the year and could ultimately enhance the overall quality of education in Dubai.
