## Sharjah Schools Implement Ethical AI Framework

The [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) Private Education Authority (SPEA) has introduced an ethical framework to guide the responsible and effective use of artificial intelligence (AI) in educational institutions across the emirate. This initiative aims to enhance the quality of education while ensuring the protection of student privacy and maintaining academic integrity.

### Goals of the Ethical AI Framework

The primary objectives of the ethical framework include promoting digital transformation within [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools), enabling both teachers and students to leverage AI technologies effectively, and establishing guidelines that protect sensitive data. The framework underscores the importance of balancing technological advancements with the need for transparency, accountability, and human oversight in the educational process.

### Alignment with National Directives

This framework is aligned with the UAE’s national directives, which emphasise ethical practices in education. The guidelines focus on key principles such as fairness, cybersecurity, and critical thinking. They also require educational institutions to verify AI-generated information and ensure that it does not replace original student work or undermine the role of educators.

### Supporting Personalised Learning and Innovation

The ethical framework is designed to foster personalised learning and encourage innovation among students. By enhancing teachers’ digital proficiency, the framework aims to equip learners with the skills necessary for future challenges. It also raises awareness about responsible AI use, data protection, and adherence to ethical practices within the educational environment.

### Responsibilities and Compliance

The framework outlines specific responsibilities for various stakeholders, including school leaders, teachers, students, IT teams, and parents. Institutions are required to implement rules for the use of AI in assignments and assessments, ensuring that students disclose AI assistance where appropriate. Additionally, the guidelines set standards for the use of AI in institutional media and promotional materials, aiming to maintain compliance with approved educational standards.

### Implications for Parents and Students

For parents considering educational options in Sharjah, the implementation of this ethical AI framework signifies a commitment to modernising the educational landscape while safeguarding their children's rights. As schools integrate AI technologies into the curriculum, parents can expect a focus on both academic excellence and the ethical implications of technology use.

This initiative reflects a broader trend in the UAE education sector, where authorities are increasingly prioritising the integration of technology in a manner that aligns with ethical standards and national directives. The establishment of such frameworks not only enhances the quality of education but also prepares students for a future where AI will play a central role in various industries.

In conclusion, the ethical AI framework introduced by the Sharjah Private Education Authority marks a significant step towards the responsible integration of technology in education. It ensures that while students benefit from advancements in AI, their privacy and academic integrity remain protected. For parents, this development is an encouraging sign of the evolving educational landscape in Sharjah.
