## New AI Guidelines for Schools in Sharjah: What Parents Should Know

[Schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) are implementing new guidelines regarding the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in education, aimed at fostering academic integrity while embracing technological advancements. As students increasingly turn to AI tools for assistance, the Sharjah Private Education Authority (SPEA) has established an ethical framework that delineates how AI should be appropriately integrated into the learning process.

Under the new guidelines, students are permitted to use AI tools such as ChatGPT for research and idea exploration; however, the use of these tools to write answers or complete assignments is strictly prohibited. The framework seeks to ensure that students produce original work and maintain academic honesty, supporting their educational journey while safeguarding their privacy and data.

### Objectives of the New AI Framework

The framework aims to enhance the quality of education and facilitate digital transformation in schools. It aligns with national directives, emphasising principles such as fairness, transparency, accountability, and critical thinking. This initiative reflects a broader commitment to integrating modern technologies into the educational landscape without compromising the integrity of student work.

Students are expected to disclose their use of AI tools when applicable and validate the information generated by these technologies. This approach not only encourages responsible use of AI but also nurtures the development of critical thinking skills among learners.

### Classroom Implementation of AI Guidelines

As schools in Sharjah begin to implement these guidelines, principals have expressed the importance of fostering independent thought in students. Schools are encouraged to create a learning environment where AI serves as a supplementary resource rather than a crutch. This involves designing assessments that gauge students' understanding beyond submitted assignments. For instance, real-time quizzes and interactive prompts may be utilised to evaluate students' grasp of concepts.

The emphasis is on ensuring that while students may use AI to gather information, the final conclusions and answers must stem from their own critical thinking and creativity. This balance aims to prepare students for a technology-driven world while preserving essential skills necessary for independent learning.

### Encouraging Responsible Use of AI

Educators in Sharjah are aware of the need to prepare students for an evolving digital landscape. Schools are developing clear expectations around the responsible use of AI, focusing on academic integrity and online safety. Teachers play a crucial role in modelling appropriate AI use, while parents are encouraged to support their children in attempting tasks independently before resorting to digital assistance.

The overarching goal of these guidelines is to cultivate students who can effectively harness AI as a tool for learning, without undermining their ability to think critically and creatively. This approach prepares students not only for academic success but also for future challenges in a rapidly changing world.

### Implications for Parents

For parents choosing schools in Sharjah, understanding these AI guidelines is essential. The new regulations signal a shift towards a more integrated approach to technology in education, prioritising student engagement and integrity. Parents can take an active role by discussing these values with their children, encouraging them to embrace technology as a means of enhancing learning rather than replacing their own efforts.

As schools navigate the complexities of AI integration, the commitment to academic honesty and critical thinking will shape the educational experience for students in Sharjah. By fostering an environment that values independent thought, schools are not only preparing students for future academic pursuits but also equipping them with the skills necessary to thrive in a technology-driven society.
