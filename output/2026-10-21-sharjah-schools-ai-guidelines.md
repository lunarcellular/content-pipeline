## Introduction of AI Guidelines in Sharjah Schools

[Schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Sharjah](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Sharjah) are adopting a new ethical framework regarding the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including popular platforms like ChatGPT. This initiative, introduced by the Sharjah Private Education Authority (SPEA), is designed to clarify how students can utilise AI in their learning while maintaining academic integrity and fostering independent thought.

## Framework for Responsible AI Use

The framework outlines specific expectations for students, educators, and parents, aiming to bolster the quality of education while supporting digital transformation. It addresses key issues such as student privacy, data protection, and the promotion of critical thinking skills. The guidelines align with national directives and values of fairness, transparency, accountability, and cybersecurity.

Students are encouraged to engage with AI tools to research and explore subjects but are explicitly prohibited from submitting AI-generated content as their own work. This policy highlights the importance of original thought, requiring students to disclose any AI use where appropriate and emphasising the necessity of verifying information produced by these technologies.

## Encouraging Academic Honesty and Critical Thinking

The primary objective of these new rules is to foster an environment of academic honesty. Schools are tasked with ensuring that students develop their own understanding and creativity, rather than relying solely on AI for answers. This approach is seen as pivotal in enhancing students' critical thinking abilities, as they are encouraged to question, analyse, and synthesise information independently.

Educators are exploring various assessment strategies to better evaluate students' understanding. For example, some schools are considering on-the-spot quizzes that require immediate responses, allowing teachers to gauge comprehension beyond merely checking homework submissions. This method aims to ensure that students can articulate their thoughts and demonstrate genuine understanding.

## Balancing Technology and Independent Learning

As AI becomes an integral part of the educational landscape, schools must find a balance between embracing these technologies and nurturing independent learning skills. Awareness of the distinction between using AI as a tool for learning and allowing it to replace personal effort is crucial. Schools are committed to teaching students how to leverage AI responsibly, ensuring it enhances rather than detracts from their educational experience.

The integration of AI is not just about technology; it involves setting expectations around academic integrity, online safety, and the accuracy of AI-generated information. Teachers are encouraged to model responsible AI use, while parents play a vital role in helping children develop independent learning habits before seeking digital assistance.

## Conclusion: Implications for Parents and Students

The introduction of AI guidelines in Sharjah schools reflects a growing recognition of the role technology plays in education. For parents, this means a continued emphasis on the importance of academic integrity and critical thinking in their children's learning experiences. As schools navigate the challenges of integrating AI into the curriculum, families are encouraged to engage with these developments, fostering an environment where technology serves as an enhancement to education rather than a substitute for traditional learning methods.

In conclusion, the new framework established by the Sharjah Private Education Authority is a proactive step towards ensuring that students are equipped to thrive in a technology-driven world, while still valuing the principles of originality and independent thought.
