## Abu Dhabi's Initiative for Emirati Teachers

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) has initiated a new Emiratisation policy aimed at placing over 2,000 UAE nationals in teaching positions within private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the emirate over the next five years. This strategic move is the result of collaboration between ADEK, the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, and the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council.

This policy introduces specific Emiratisation targets for the private education sector in Abu Dhabi, complementing existing federal requirements. The initiative is designed not only to enhance the representation of Emiratis in teaching roles but also to contribute to the overall quality of education in the emirate.

## Strategic Partnerships with Private Education Operators

As part of this initiative, five major private education operators have formed strategic partnerships with ADEK, committing to recruit a significant number of Emirati educators. The targeted numbers include commitments from various operators to hire over 200 Emirati educators, which reflects a robust effort to enrich the educational landscape with local talent.

The drive will initially focus on key subjects such as Social Studies, Arabic, and Islamic Studies. Recruitment will unfold gradually, providing ample opportunity for these new educators to integrate into the teaching workforce effectively.

## Educational Programmes for Aspiring Emirati Teachers

To facilitate this initiative, ADEK has introduced several entry and development programmes aimed at UAE nationals aspiring to enter the teaching profession. The Midad short-term programme, launched recently, serves as an entry point for individuals exploring teaching careers. In addition, the Kon Moallim programme provides a sponsored postgraduate diploma in education, enhancing the qualifications of participants.

In collaboration with the Emirates College for Advanced Education (ECAE), the Teachers Programme has also been established. This programme offers two pathways—Teacher and Teacher Assistant—enabling participants to gain the necessary skills and experience for successful teaching careers. Participants in the Teacher pathway may receive financial incentives during their training, supporting their professional development.

## Framework for Professional Standards

The initiative is anchored in the Abu Dhabi Educators' Proficiency Framework, which has been developed jointly by ADEK and ECAE. This framework outlines the professional values and standards expected of educators in the emirate, ensuring that the teaching workforce is not only well-trained but also aligned with the educational goals of Abu Dhabi.

Additionally, the Emirati Student Employment Contract programme offers a unique opportunity for UAE nationals, allowing them to begin working with an employer while still studying. This programme not only provides a monthly salary and pension registration but also counts toward the employer's Emiratisation targets, thus reinforcing the importance of local talent in the education sector.

## Implications for Parents

This initiative has significant implications for parents in Abu Dhabi, particularly those with children in private schools. The increase in Emirati teachers could contribute to a more culturally relevant and contextually informed education for students. As local educators bring their experiences and perspectives into the classroom, parents can expect a curriculum that better reflects the values and aspirations of the UAE.

Furthermore, as schools work towards meeting these Emiratisation targets, they may also enhance their community engagement, creating a stronger connection between educational institutions and Emirati families. This initiative not only aims to bolster the teaching workforce but also seeks to enrich the educational experience for students across Abu Dhabi. 

In conclusion, ADEK's Emiratisation policy marks a proactive step towards fostering a more inclusive and representative teaching environment in private schools. This effort aligns with broader national goals of enhancing local employment and ensuring that Emirati voices are reflected in all facets of education.
