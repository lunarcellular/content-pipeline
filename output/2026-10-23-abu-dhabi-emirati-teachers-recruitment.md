## Abu Dhabi's Initiative for Emirati Teachers

[Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) is set to welcome over 2,000 Emiratis into its private school teaching workforce over the next five years under a new Emiratisation initiative launched by the Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK). This significant move aims to enhance the representation of UAE Nationals in the education sector, aligning with broader national objectives.

## Understanding the Emiratisation Policy

The new Emiratisation policy specifically targets teaching roles within Abu Dhabi's private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools). It is supported by various government bodies, including the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) and the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council (NAFIS). The initiative seeks to create a robust education system led by high-quality teaching talent, prioritising the wellbeing of students and their connection to Emirati heritage and identity.

ADEK's initiative complements existing Emiratisation requirements at the federal level, establishing a dedicated approach to increase Emirati representation in classrooms. The policy is anchored in the Abu Dhabi Educators’ Proficiency Framework, which outlines the values and skills expected of educators in the emirate. These include cultural respect, ethical responsibility, and a commitment to lifelong learning and innovation.

## Recruitment Commitments from Schools

Under this new policy, five major private education operators have made recruitment commitments to create hundreds of opportunities for Emirati educators. The target roles will primarily focus on subjects such as Social Studies, Arabic, and Islamic Studies. The recruitment process will occur gradually over the next five years, allowing schools to adapt and integrate the new educators effectively.

This strategic approach not only aids in fulfilling Emiratisation targets but also provides a clearer framework for schools regarding the recruitment and training of Emirati teachers. The collaboration between ADEK and these private education operators aims to foster an inclusive learning environment, particularly for Students of Determination, by ensuring access to qualified professionals who can provide tailored support.

## Benefits for Emirati Educators

The initiative introduces the Emirati Student Employment Contract programme, enabling Emirati students to join an employer while studying. This programme offers financial support, a monthly salary, pension registration, and practical on-the-job training. Such measures are designed to support students while they complete their education and help them transition smoothly into the workforce.

Moreover, ADEK is collaborating with MOHRE and NAFIS to build a sustainable talent supply that meets the requirements of this policy. This includes identifying and preparing potential candidates through various teacher attraction and development pathways, ensuring the long-term success of Emiratisation in the education sector.

## Implications for Parents and Schools

For parents considering private education for their children in Abu Dhabi, the introduction of a larger Emirati teaching workforce could enhance the educational experience. With more Emirati teachers, schools may offer a curriculum that is better aligned with local cultural values and perspectives. This development could also lead to improved support systems for Students of Determination, fostering a more inclusive educational environment.

The commitment to recruiting Emirati teachers is a significant step towards creating a more representative and culturally aware educational landscape in Abu Dhabi. As schools adapt to these changes, parents can expect to see a stronger emphasis on local identity and values within the curriculum, which could positively influence their children's education and overall school experience. 

In conclusion, as Abu Dhabi moves forward with this ambitious initiative, the focus on Emiratisation in education will play a crucial role in shaping the future of teaching and learning in the emirate, ultimately benefiting both students and the wider community.
