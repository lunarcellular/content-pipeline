## Overview of the Emiratisation Initiative

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) has unveiled an ambitious Emiratisation policy aimed at placing over 2,000 Emirati nationals in teaching roles across the emirate's private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) within the next five years. This initiative, developed in collaboration with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation and the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council, seeks to enhance the representation of Emiratis in the education sector.

## The Goals of the Policy

The Emiratisation policy outlines specific targets for the teaching profession, complementing existing federal requirements. The initial focus will be on recruiting educators for crucial subjects such as Social Studies, Arabic, and Islamic Studies. As part of this phased recruitment, multiple private education operators have committed to hiring Emirati teachers, with several organisations pledging significant numbers of new hires.

## Partnerships with Private Education Operators

Five notable private education operators have entered strategic partnerships with ADEK to meet these goals. These partnerships include commitments ranging from over 200 Emirati educators by one operator to smaller targets by others, such as more than 150 and 120 teachers. This collaborative effort underlines the importance of a united approach to strengthening the Emiratisation initiative in the private education sector.

## Support Programs for Emirati Educators

ADEK is also introducing various entry and development programmes tailored for UAE nationals aspiring to become teachers. One such initiative is the Midad programme, launched earlier in 2026, designed as an entry point for those exploring teaching careers. Additionally, the Kon Moallim programme, set to begin in 2025, offers a sponsored postgraduate diploma in education in partnership with a local educational institution.

The Teachers Programme, also in collaboration with the aforementioned institution, provides two pathways: Teacher and Teacher Assistant. Participants in the Teacher pathway may receive financial incentives during their training, further encouraging Emiratis to join the teaching profession.

## Framework for Professional Standards

The new Emiratisation policy is anchored in the Abu Dhabi Educators' Proficiency Framework, jointly developed by ADEK and a local educational institution. This framework establishes the professional values and standards expected of educators in Abu Dhabi, ensuring that new recruits meet the necessary qualifications and competencies.

## Employment Opportunities for Emirati Students

UAE nationals can also benefit from the Emirati Student Employment Contract programme, which allows them to work with educational institutions while they study. This programme offers participants a monthly salary, pension registration, and on-the-job training, contributing to the employer's Emiratisation targets while providing valuable experience to the students.

## Context and Implications for Parents

The introduction of this Emiratisation policy is significant for parents considering private education options in Abu Dhabi. It not only aims to increase the number of Emirati teachers—thereby enhancing cultural representation in schools—but also ensures that educational standards are maintained through a structured framework for teacher qualifications.

As the education landscape evolves, parents may find that schools in Abu Dhabi are increasingly focused on integrating Emirati values and perspectives within their curricula, reflecting the broader goals of the Emiratisation initiative. This shift could lead to a more culturally responsive education system, benefiting both Emirati students and the wider student population.

In summary, while the policy’s ultimate success will depend on effective implementation and collaboration among private education operators, it marks a significant step toward enhancing the participation of Emiratis in the teaching profession within Abu Dhabi's private schools.
