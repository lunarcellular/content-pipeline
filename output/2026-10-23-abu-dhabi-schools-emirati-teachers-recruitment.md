## New ADEK Policy to Increase Emiratisation in Abu Dhabi Schools

[Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi)'s private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are set to boost their teaching workforce by recruiting over 2,000 Emirati teachers over the next five years as part of a new Emiratisation policy introduced by the Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK). This initiative aims to enhance the representation of UAE Nationals in educational roles, aligning with broader national objectives to develop a high-quality education system.

The new policy outlines specific Emiratisation targets for teaching positions within Abu Dhabi's private education sector. It supports ongoing efforts to ensure that the teaching workforce reflects the values and cultural identity of the UAE, thereby fostering a more connected and engaged learning environment for students.

## Strategic Partnerships and Support

The Emiratisation initiative is supported by several key stakeholders, including the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) and the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council (NAFIS), alongside five major private education operators in the region. These partnerships are designed to facilitate recruitment processes, provide access to qualified national talent, and ensure that schools are equipped with the necessary resources to meet the new targets.

As part of this policy, ADEK will collaborate with private schools to enhance their capacity to integrate Emirati educators into their staff. This includes establishing structured pathways into employment, offering practical training, and providing funded qualification opportunities tailored to the UAE's educational landscape.

## Focus on Key Subjects

The recruitment drive is expected to concentrate initially on critical subjects such as Social Studies, Arabic, and Islamic Studies. This targeted approach aims to ensure that Emirati teachers are placed in roles that align with their cultural expertise and national identity, which is essential for nurturing a sense of belonging among students.

## Long-Term Commitment to Emiratisation

ADEK's commitment to building a robust teaching workforce is further evidenced by its recent agreements with various educational bodies, which include plans to create more than 300 opportunities for Emirati educators. The new policy complements existing federal Emiratisation requirements while providing a dedicated framework for increasing Emirati representation in the classroom.

The initiative is also part of a wider strategy to enhance the quality of education in Abu Dhabi, equipping students with essential skills and knowledge that prepare them for future challenges. By fostering a teaching environment led by Emirati educators, the policy aims to instill a sense of pride in local heritage and culture among students.

## Implications for Parents

For parents considering private education in Abu Dhabi, the increased recruitment of Emirati teachers signifies a commitment to enhancing the educational experience for their children. The policy promises to create a more inclusive learning environment that not only supports academic achievement but also prioritises cultural education and wellbeing.

As the teaching landscape evolves with this new policy, parents can expect to see a greater emphasis on subjects that reflect local values and traditions, ultimately enriching their children’s education and personal development. This strategic move towards Emiratisation in education highlights the ongoing efforts to empower UAE Nationals and ensure that the future of education in Abu Dhabi is both sustainable and culturally relevant.
