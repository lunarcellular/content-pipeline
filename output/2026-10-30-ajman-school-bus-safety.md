## Enhanced Safety Features for School Buses in Ajman

In a significant move to bolster student safety, every school bus operating in Ajman will now be equipped with automatic fire suppression systems. This decision has been confirmed by the emirate's transport authority, which aims to enhance the safety standards of student transport.

The new fire suppression systems are specifically designed to respond swiftly to any potential fires within the engine compartment. This proactive measure helps to contain flames and significantly reduces the risk posed to pupils, drivers, and bus supervisors during their daily commutes to and from school.

### Commitment to Safety Standards

The executive director of the Public Transport and Licensing Agency at the Ajman Transport Authority highlighted that achieving full compliance with safety requirements is a top priority. Regular checks will be conducted to ensure that all buses and their fire protection systems are operational and ready for service. This initiative not only aims to enhance the safety of transport services but also seeks to strengthen parents' confidence in the reliability of the school bus network in Ajman.

By implementing these fire suppression systems, the authority underscores its commitment to prioritising pupils’ safety. The introduction of these systems is part of a broader strategy to improve overall safety and fire prevention standards across the emirate’s school transport services.
