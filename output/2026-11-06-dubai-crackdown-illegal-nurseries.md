## Overview of the Situation

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries?location=Dubai) Police have recently taken significant action against unlicensed [nurseries](https://www.schoolsery.com/nurseries) in the emirate, arresting 34 individuals linked to 33 illegal childcare facilities. These operations were found to be putting children's safety at risk by failing to meet essential hygiene and safety standards. Many of these nurseries were located in residential buildings, not designed nor equipped for childcare, and were often targeted at low-income families by offering lower fees than licensed alternatives.

## The Nature of the Violations

The illegal nurseries exploited residential spaces to operate outside the established regulatory framework, which is intended to protect children. The authorities highlighted that these facilities not only lacked proper licensing but also operated in unsanitary conditions. Concerns included inadequate safety measures, which raised the potential for neglect and abuse.

The crackdown was the result of coordinated efforts involving Dubai Police, Dubai Municipality, the Community Development Authority, and the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism. Their joint campaign aimed to bolster child protection and eliminate potential hazards related to unregulated childcare practices.

## Response by Authorities

Following investigations and field inspections, police were able to identify and raid these unlicensed nurseries. Officers conducted surveillance based on tips received regarding the illegal operations, leading to the arrests and a broader crackdown on non-compliant childcare providers. Authorities have emphasized that all childcare facilities must adhere to licensing requirements to ensure the safety and well-being of the children in their care.

Dubai Police issued warnings about the dangers posed by these illegal nurseries, underscoring the lack of institutional oversight over staff and the facilities themselves. The police urged parents to be vigilant and to prioritise their children’s health and safety over cost when selecting childcare options.

## Advice for Parents

In light of these developments, parents are advised to take several precautions when choosing a nursery for their children. They should verify that any facility they consider is officially licensed and compliant with local regulations. The emphasis is on ensuring that affordability does not overshadow the importance of a safe and secure environment for children.

Additionally, parents are encouraged to report any suspicious activities related to unlicensed nurseries or childcare services. Dubai Police have made it clear that community vigilance is crucial in identifying and addressing illegal operations. Reports can be made directly through the Dubai Police call centre or their official website.

## Conclusion

The recent arrests related to unlicensed nurseries in Dubai serve as a critical reminder for parents about the importance of compliance with childcare regulations. The safety and welfare of children should always be the primary concern when selecting a nursery. As authorities continue to crack down on illegal operations, it is essential for parents to remain informed and proactive in evaluating their childcare options.

By taking these precautions, parents can help ensure a safe and nurturing environment for their children in Dubai's childcare landscape.
