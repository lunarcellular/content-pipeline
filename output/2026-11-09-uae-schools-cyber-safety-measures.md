## Enhanced Cyber Safety Measures in UAE Schools

Private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) across the UAE are implementing stronger cybersecurity measures as part of a new initiative aimed at safeguarding students from various online risks. These measures, which have been reinforced during the academic year 2026–2027, include stricter internet filtering, heightened controls over digital platforms, and updated safeguarding policies specifically designed to prevent cyberbullying and exposure to inappropriate content.

Schools are now closely monitoring internet activity on their networks, restricting access to unsuitable websites and videos, and providing students with training to help them recognise harmful online behaviour. This initiative underscores the importance of not solely relying on technology to protect children but also emphasises the role of parental involvement and responsible digital behaviour.

## Stricter Internet Controls

To bolster online safety, schools in the UAE are enforcing stricter internet controls aimed at blocking access to inappropriate search results and video content. These controls are designed to address gaps that may not be covered by traditional filtering systems, allowing schools to better protect students from harmful online material.

For instance, some schools have adopted advanced filtering systems that work on encrypted websites, implementing enforced SafeSearch, strict YouTube controls, and custom content blocking. Additionally, students are prohibited from using personal mobile phones on school premises, with these devices also being blocked from accessing the school's Wi-Fi network.

## Updated Safeguarding Policies

In line with the Knowledge and Human Development Authority's (KHDA) expectations, many schools have updated their Anti-Bullying and Cyber Safety Policies. These revisions aim to address inappropriate online behaviour and misuse of communication platforms. Measures include using firewall protection and designating Cyber Safety In-charges to oversee online conduct. 

Schools have established tiered behaviour frameworks guiding responses to incidents, ranging from preventive education to more intensive interventions. The most common issues reported involve inappropriate online behaviours rather than illegal activities, such as unkind comments in group chats or sharing images without consent.

## Monitoring and Reporting Incidents

Schools are also vigilant in monitoring signs that may indicate a student is experiencing online difficulties, such as withdrawal, anxiety, or reluctance to attend school. Each reported incident is taken seriously and investigated thoroughly, with students interviewed separately to maintain confidentiality and support.

It is acknowledged that many cases of cyberbullying often originate from personal devices outside school hours, complicating the detection and management of such incidents. Schools encourage students, parents, and staff to report any concerns, fostering a culture of openness and vigilance.

## Evolving Digital Threats

As digital threats continue to evolve, schools are actively reviewing and updating their safeguarding procedures to address new risks associated with social media, artificial intelligence, and online interactions. Beyond simply implementing restrictions, there is a significant focus on educating students about online threats and encouraging them to seek help when encountering harmful content.

This proactive approach indicates a shift towards a more comprehensive understanding of cybersecurity in education, with schools aiming to equip students with the knowledge and skills needed to navigate the digital landscape safely.

## Implications for Parents

For parents choosing schools in the UAE, these enhanced cybersecurity measures are a crucial consideration. As schools place greater emphasis on internet safety and digital citizenship, parents may find reassurance in the proactive steps being taken to protect their children online. The focus on education, alongside technological safeguards, highlights the commitment of schools to fostering a safe learning environment.

Overall, as the landscape of online threats changes, the response from schools reflects a growing awareness of the need for robust cybersecurity practices. Parents should engage with schools to understand their specific policies and the measures in place to ensure their children's safety in an increasingly digital world.
