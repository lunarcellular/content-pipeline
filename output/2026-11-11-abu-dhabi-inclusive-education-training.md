## Abu Dhabi's New Inclusive Education Initiative

The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Abu%20Dhabi) Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) has launched a specialised training programme aimed at enhancing inclusive education within the emirate. This initiative is designed to equip Emirati professionals with the necessary skills to support students with diverse educational needs. The programme will facilitate the training of up to 250 Emiratis annually, promoting inclusive educational practices and career development within the sector.

## Key Features of the Programme

The partnership between ADEK and PureHealth, a prominent healthcare group in the Middle East, is central to this initiative. By integrating educational methodologies with healthcare expertise, the programme aims to create a robust framework for inclusive education. Participants will undergo a comprehensive six-month training programme that includes both theoretical instruction and practical experience in classroom settings.

### Training Components

The training curriculum encompasses various disciplines, including psychology, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy, and behavioural support. This multidisciplinary approach ensures that participants develop a well-rounded skill set, enabling them to address the unique needs of students effectively. 

### Career Pathways for Emiratis

In line with the Emiratisation efforts, the programme not only provides foundational training for unemployed Emiratis but also offers advanced skills development for those already employed as inclusion assistants. This dual focus supports career progression, allowing participants to advance into roles such as qualified inclusion teachers. 

ADEK has indicated that the initiative is part of broader efforts to enhance the quality of education in Abu Dhabi. The programme will certify up to 120 new inclusion assistants each year while upgrading the skills of existing assistants, thereby building a qualified workforce to support inclusive education.

## Importance of Inclusive Education

The importance of this initiative extends beyond individual career growth. By fostering an inclusive educational environment, the programme aims to ensure that all students, regardless of their needs, have access to quality education and the opportunity to thrive. This aligns with ADEK’s commitment to creating educational frameworks that support the learning and development of every student in the emirate.

The collaboration between ADEK and PureHealth is particularly noteworthy as it highlights the need for integrated approaches in education. By addressing both educational and health-related aspects of student development, the initiative seeks to identify individual needs early and establish supportive learning environments.

## Implications for Parents

For parents considering [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in Abu Dhabi, this initiative signifies a commitment to inclusive education. Parents can expect schools to be better equipped in terms of resources and trained staff to support children with diverse learning requirements. This could lead to enhanced educational experiences for students, fostering an environment where all learners can achieve their full potential.

In summary, the new training programme for inclusive education marks a significant step toward improving educational outcomes for all students in Abu Dhabi. By investing in Emirati talent and integrating healthcare expertise, this initiative not only prepares individuals for rewarding careers but also strengthens the educational landscape within the emirate.
