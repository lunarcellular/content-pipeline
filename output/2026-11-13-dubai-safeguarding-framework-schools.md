## Introduction

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) has unveiled a new unified safeguarding and learner protection framework aimed at enhancing the safety and wellbeing of students across private educational institutions, including [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) and higher education centres. Developed by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), this framework addresses critical issues such as bullying, neglect, exploitation, and cyberbullying.

## Framework Overview

The Policy Framework for Safeguarding and Learner Protection establishes common standards designed to protect learners from various forms of harm. Notably, it extends its protective measures to individuals aged 18 and above, recognising that safeguarding responsibilities should encompass all learners, not just those in traditional school settings.

### Implementation Timeline

The framework will be rolled out in phases, allowing educational institutions a one-year grace period to comply with its requirements. The first phase includes familiarisation sessions for different educational sectors, scheduled for November to December 2026. This is followed by the introduction of additional guidance, templates, and resources in the first quarter of 2027.

A significant component of the new framework is the planned launch of an automated case management system. This system, set to go live in early 2027, aims to standardise the reporting and management of safeguarding concerns across the educational sector.

### Key Features

The framework places a strong emphasis on accountability, extending responsibilities for safeguarding to all staff, including teachers, supervisors, and even transport personnel. Each institution will appoint a designated safeguarding governor tasked with ensuring that protection remains a priority within the governing body.

The framework also aims to enhance awareness among staff, particularly those involved in student wellbeing. Training programmes will be rolled out to equip staff with the necessary skills to identify and respond to safeguarding concerns effectively.

## Importance for Parents

This new safeguarding framework represents a significant step forward in ensuring the safety and wellbeing of students in Dubai's private education sector. For parents, this development is reassuring, as it establishes a regulatory basis for safeguarding that aims to create safer educational environments for their children.

By implementing standardised procedures and enhancing accountability, parents can expect institutions to take proactive measures in addressing potential risks. The focus on comprehensive training for staff means that educational environments will not only be safer but also more supportive of students' overall wellbeing.

### Conclusion

The introduction of the safeguarding framework in Dubai is a positive stride towards creating a more secure educational landscape. The phased implementation allows schools ample time to adapt to the new requirements, ensuring that the measures taken are effective and in line with the unique culture of each institution. As these changes take shape, parents can feel more confident in the protections and support systems that will be in place for their children.
