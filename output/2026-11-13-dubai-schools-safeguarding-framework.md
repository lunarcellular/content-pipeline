## Introduction of the Safeguarding Framework

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)’s private educational institutions are set to adopt a new safeguarding framework aimed at enhancing learner protection. The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has established this comprehensive policy to address a range of safeguarding concerns, ensuring that educational settings are safer and more responsive to the needs of young learners.

## Key Elements of the Framework

The safeguarding framework introduces mandatory requirements that all private educational institutions must follow. It establishes a set of clear standards focusing on four primary areas:

1. **Prevention**: Institutions are tasked with creating safer learning environments and reducing potential risks.
2. **Early Identification**: The framework emphasizes the importance of recognising warning signs and safeguarding concerns as early as possible.
3. **Reporting and Response**: [Schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) must ensure that any concerns raised are promptly reported, escalated, and addressed efficiently.
4. **Accountability**: Institutions will be held responsible for adhering to these safeguarding practices, with regular monitoring and compliance checks.

This framework will be enforced over a phased implementation period of up to 12 months, allowing schools to adjust their policies and practices accordingly.

## Scope and Applicability

The new safeguarding framework is applicable to all KHDA-licensed and permitted private educational institutions in Dubai. This includes early childhood centres, private schools, higher education facilities, and vocational training institutes. It encompasses both in-person and virtual learning environments and is designed to protect learners aged 21 and under. Special provisions are included for those aged 18 to 21, requiring institutions to identify concerns and provide necessary support.

## Addressing a Range of Risks

The framework specifically targets various risks that affect learners' safety and wellbeing. Among these are issues related to:

- Online grooming and harmful digital content
- Misuse of technology, including artificial intelligence
- Safety risks during transport, sports, educational visits, and extracurricular activities
- Wellbeing concerns that might indicate a risk of harm

Institutions are required to implement appropriate digital safety controls and provide age-appropriate education on online safety.

## Enhanced Responsibilities for Educational Institutions

Under the new framework, the responsibility for safeguarding is not limited to designated staff members but must be integrated into the overall policies and practices of the institution. Key roles established within each institution include:

- **Safeguarding Governor**: A designated individual at the board level who will provide independent oversight and monitor compliance.
- **Head of the Institution**: The principal or head will have executive accountability and will be directly answerable to KHDA.
- **Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)**: Each institution must appoint a DSL to coordinate safeguarding concerns and responses, supported by deputies.

These roles are designed to strengthen accountability and ensure that safeguarding measures are taken seriously throughout the institution.

## Reporting Protocols and Procedures

The framework outlines strict protocols for reporting safeguarding concerns. Staff members are required to report any reasonable suspicion or information that raises concerns, without needing conclusive evidence. This proactive approach is essential in protecting learners and ensuring their safety.

Institutions must provide accessible reporting channels for both staff and learners, enabling them to raise concerns confidentially. Furthermore, schools are urged to regularly review their safeguarding data and conduct comprehensive annual reviews to assess the effectiveness of their practices.

## Conclusion: Implications for Parents

This new safeguarding framework represents a significant step forward in ensuring the safety and wellbeing of learners in Dubai schools. For parents, it underscores the importance of choosing educational institutions that prioritise safeguarding and compliance with established standards. As schools implement these changes, parents can expect enhanced transparency and accountability regarding their children's safety in both physical and digital learning environments.

By fostering a culture of safety and responsiveness, Dubai’s educational landscape is poised to better protect learners and support their overall development.
