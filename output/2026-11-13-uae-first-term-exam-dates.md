## Exam Schedule and Requirements

The UAE Ministry of Education has officially announced the timetable for first-term examinations for the academic year 2026–2027. Students in Grades 3 to 12 will attend school in person for exams scheduled from **25 November to 11 December**. This timetable applies to all government [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) and private institutions adhering to the ministry's curriculum.

## Examination Details

The examination period will commence with Grade 12 pupils in both general and advanced streams beginning with mathematics on **25 November**. Following this, students will take English and chemistry examinations on **26** and **27 November**, respectively. After a break, examinations will resume on **7 December** with physics, and continue with Arabic, Islamic Education, and social studies on the subsequent days, concluding with biology on **11 December**.

For Grade 12 pupils enrolled in the applied stream, the first exam will also be on **25 November**, starting with applied mathematics, followed by English and applied sciences on later dates. These students will take Arabic, Islamic Education, and social studies on **8, 9, and 10 December**, respectively.

Students in the general and advanced streams will be required to sit for eight subjects, while those in the applied stream will have six subjects to complete.

## In-Person Attendance and Exam Format

The ministry has mandated in-person attendance for all examinations, which means that students must be present at their respective schools during this period. Additionally, pupils are required to bring their personal laptops to the school for the exams. 

For Grades 3 and 4, the exams will be conducted in a paper-based format, while students in Grades 5 to 12 will experience a mix of written and electronic assessments based on the subject matter.

Grade 12 English examinations will include both writing and reading components, with the writing being conducted electronically. In contrast, the Arabic exams will consist of a combination of paper-based writing and electronic reading assessments.

Importantly, teachers are prohibited from reading the examination questions aloud, except for those in Grade 3, who may receive assistance. However, any guidance that could reveal answers is strictly forbidden.

## Key Deadlines and Regulations

An important deadline has been set for **20 November**, by which schools must complete and approve student assessment grades for all subjects in Groups A and B. This is critical to ensure that all students are adequately prepared for the upcoming examinations.

In light of these updates, parents should remain informed about their children's examination schedules and the requirements for in-person attendance. This new timetable signifies a structured approach to assessments, ensuring that students are well-prepared for their academic progress.
