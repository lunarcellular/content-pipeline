## Introduction of New Attendance Reporting Rules

[Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) are now required to report students who miss five consecutive school days without a valid explanation. This initiative, introduced by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), aims to enhance the safeguarding of students and identify those who may be at risk due to unexplained absences. 

## Key Provisions of the Attendance Policy

Under the new attendance regulations, schools must also notify authorities if unexplained absences account for 10 per cent or more of a pupil's total school days within a term. These measures are part of the KHDA's Policy Framework for Safeguarding and Learner Protection, which places a heightened responsibility on educational institutions to ensure the safety and well-being of their students.

### Immediate Reporting Protocols

The framework emphasises that schools should not wait until the established reporting thresholds are reached if there are concerns regarding a child's welfare. Staff are required to act promptly and refer any concerns to the school's designated safeguarding personnel. This proactive approach is intended to prevent potential issues before they escalate.

### Attendance Monitoring and Support

In addition to the reporting requirements, schools must maintain accurate attendance records and investigate patterns of repeated or unexplained absences. This includes monitoring occurrences such as frequent visits to the bathroom or school clinic, which could indicate underlying issues that may require intervention.

For students under 18, schools are mandated to follow up with parents, guardians, or caregivers to ascertain the reasons for absences. Students aged 18 and older will be contacted directly, with family involvement only occurring if the student consents or if there is a risk of harm.

## Broader Impact on Educational Institutions

The attendance regulations also extend to universities, vocational training institutions, and early childhood centres, although the specific requirements regarding attendance reporting may vary by institution type. For instance, universities and vocational providers are not bound by the same five-day and 10 per cent thresholds; instead, they must establish their own criteria reflecting different study patterns, including part-time or distance learning courses.

Early childhood centres are tasked with calculating absences based on individual enrolment days rather than the centre's overall operational calendar. Furthermore, schools offering boarding facilities must adhere to additional requirements, including appointing a residential safeguarding lead and ensuring that existing boarding staff complete necessary safeguarding training.

## Significance of the New Measures

These new attendance reporting measures are crucial for ensuring that unexplained absences are not merely treated as administrative concerns. They serve as a vital recognition of potential underlying issues that could indicate a child is facing difficulties or may be at risk of harm. 

For parents, this heightened awareness and proactive approach by schools can provide reassurance that educational institutions are taking their children's safety seriously. As concerns about student well-being continue to rise globally, such measures reflect a growing commitment within the UAE's educational sector to ensure a secure and supportive environment for all students.

In conclusion, the introduction of these attendance reporting requirements marks a significant step forward in safeguarding the well-being of students in Dubai schools. The emphasis on early intervention and thorough monitoring is pivotal in fostering a safe educational landscape, ultimately benefiting both students and parents alike.
