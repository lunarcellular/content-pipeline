## New Attendance Reporting Requirements for Dubai Schools

Private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) are now mandated to report student absences under new regulations from the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA). The updated attendance policy requires schools to notify the relevant authorities when a student has missed five consecutive school days without a valid reason, or when unexplained absences accumulate to 10 per cent of the total school days in a term.

The KHDA has implemented these measures under its Policy Framework for Safeguarding and Learner Protection, which aims to enhance student safety and wellbeing by linking attendance monitoring to the early detection of risks. This systematic approach is designed to ensure that schools can take necessary actions to protect students at the earliest signs of absence.

### Key Reporting Thresholds

The new reporting requirements stipulate that schools must act when either of two thresholds is met: 
1. A student misses five consecutive days of school.
2. A student accrues absences that total 10 per cent or more of the term days.

Schools are also obligated to respond immediately to any attendance concerns that suggest a student may be at risk, even if the specific reporting thresholds have not been reached. This proactive stance reinforces the importance of student safety and ensures that schools remain vigilant in monitoring attendance patterns.

### Establishing Attendance Policies

Under these new regulations, educational institutions are required to develop written attendance policies that are suitable for the age and educational needs of their students. Schools must maintain accurate records of attendance, investigate repeated or unexplained absences, and identify any patterns that may indicate a student needs additional support.

Furthermore, staff members are instructed to alert the designated safeguarding lead at their institution whenever there is a concern regarding a student's attendance. This immediate response is crucial in addressing potential risks and ensuring that students receive appropriate support.

### Support for Vulnerable Students

The policy also allows schools to set lower reporting thresholds for individual students who are recognised as having vulnerabilities, enabling earlier intervention when necessary. For students under the age of 18, schools are required to communicate attendance concerns with parents or guardians, while students aged 18 and over should be contacted directly, involving their families only with consent or if there is a risk of harm.

### Protocol for Missing Students

In addition to the attendance reporting requirements, all educational institutions must establish clear procedures to follow when a student cannot be accounted for during school hours. These procedures should ensure that any incidents of missing students are managed immediately and escalate to safeguarding personnel, school leadership, and, if necessary, parents and relevant authorities.

Institutions are also tasked with creating written procedures for handling such incidents and testing their effectiveness within a 12-month implementation period. This comprehensive approach aims to enhance the safeguarding framework across Dubai schools.

### Implications for Parents

For parents, these new attendance regulations underscore the importance of monitoring their child's school attendance closely. The KHDA's robust framework aims to foster a safe educational environment where potential issues can be addressed swiftly. Parents should be aware of the reporting thresholds and ensure that they communicate with their children's schools regarding any attendance concerns.

These regulations reflect a growing emphasis on student safety and wellbeing within the education sector in Dubai. By establishing clear protocols for attendance monitoring and intervention, the KHDA is taking significant steps to protect students and promote a supportive learning environment.

In summary, the introduction of mandatory attendance reporting in Dubai schools is a critical development aimed at safeguarding students and ensuring their wellbeing. As these regulations take effect, parents, educators, and school authorities must collaborate to create a nurturing educational atmosphere for all students.
