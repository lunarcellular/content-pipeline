## New Communication Policy for Teachers in Dubai Schools

In a significant move to enhance student safety, [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai)'s Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) has implemented a new communication policy that prohibits teachers from using personal devices and social media to contact students. This policy is part of a broader initiative aimed at establishing consistent safeguarding standards across Dubai's private education sector.

### Key Features of the Policy

Announced on November 13, the policy mandates that all interactions between educational staff and students must occur through institutionally approved devices and platforms. This includes a ban on the use of personal mobile phones, WhatsApp, and social media accounts for any communication with learners. The intention behind this measure is to maintain clear professional boundaries and ensure a safe environment for students.

The framework outlines several critical components aimed at promoting safe working practices. For instance, educators must not engage in secretive relationships with students, use personal contact details, or accept unauthorised gifts. Additionally, they are required to conduct interactions in open, visible environments whenever possible, which further reinforces accountability.

### Comprehensive Coverage and Implementation

The new guidelines extend beyond teachers to include all adults working within educational institutions, such as coaches, volunteers, and third-party contractors. Every adult involved with learners is required to acknowledge and adhere to the institution's Code of Conduct during their induction. This comprehensive approach ensures that safeguarding responsibilities encompass all interactions with students, including those aged 18 and above, with provisions adapted for adult consent and confidentiality.

The KHDA has recognised that while many institutions already had safeguarding policies in place, a unified framework across the sector is essential. The new policy addresses various risks, including cyberbullying, online grooming, and digital coercion, as well as the misuse of artificial intelligence in educational settings.

Institutions are also required to appoint a safeguarding governor at the board level and a designated safeguarding lead or child protection coordinator to oversee compliance with these standards. This structured oversight is critical for the ongoing assessment of staff suitability throughout employment, allowing for timely action when concerns arise.

### Implementation Timeline and Expectations

The implementation of this policy will occur over a phased period of up to 12 months, allowing educational institutions adequate time to update their policies, train staff, and refine their safeguarding procedures. This gradual approach is designed to facilitate a smooth transition to the new standards while ensuring that all stakeholders are adequately prepared.

### Implications for Parents and Students

For parents considering educational options in Dubai, this new communication policy represents a significant commitment to student safety and welfare. The emphasis on clear communication channels and professional boundaries is likely to enhance trust between families and educational institutions. It also demonstrates a proactive approach to safeguarding, which is increasingly crucial in today’s digital landscape.

As [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) adapt to these new guidelines, parents can expect to see enhanced transparency in communication between educators and students, fostering a safer and more supportive learning environment. The unified standards set forth by the KHDA are a vital step toward ensuring that all educational institutions in Dubai uphold the highest levels of protection for their students.

In conclusion, the KHDA's new communication policy not only aims to safeguard students but also reflects a broader commitment to enhancing the quality of education in Dubai. As these principles are put into practice, they are expected to influence the overall landscape of private education in the emirate, aligning it with international best practices in student protection and educator conduct.
