## New Reporting Requirements Introduced

Private [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) are now mandated to report student absences under a new safeguarding framework established by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA). This initiative aims to enhance the monitoring of student attendance, thereby identifying potential risks related to children's safety and wellbeing.

The updated regulations require schools to notify KHDA if a student has missed five consecutive school days without a valid excuse or if their unexplained absences accumulate to 10 per cent of total school days within a term. Schools must act promptly upon reaching these thresholds, as waiting until the end of a term to highlight attendance patterns is no longer acceptable. 

## Purpose of the Framework

The primary goal of this framework is to address safeguarding concerns immediately. If schools suspect any potential risk to a child's safety, they are encouraged to take action without delay, regardless of whether the established thresholds have been met.

### Attendance Monitoring Procedures

To comply with these requirements, schools must develop comprehensive attendance policies and maintain accurate records of student attendance daily. These records can be kept in either digital or physical formats, but they must be reliable and updated consistently throughout the school day. Additionally, schools are tasked with monitoring punctuality and attendance across all educational phases. 

In instances of repeated absences, schools are instructed to engage with parents or guardians for students under 18 years of age. For those aged 18 and above, schools may directly contact the students, with parental involvement permitted only with the student's consent or in cases where there is a risk of harm. Documentation of all communications and follow-up actions is essential, ensuring that any concerns are referred to the school's Child Protection Coordinator or Designated Safeguarding Lead.

## Supporting Students

The framework also encourages schools to look for additional warning signs that may accompany repeated absences. These could manifest as sudden changes in behaviour, declining academic performance, or other concerns regarding a student's overall wellbeing. Schools are urged to implement supportive measures, including mentoring, pastoral care, and the appointment of dedicated attendance leads. 

In situations where absences are linked to broader family or social challenges, schools may collaborate with external agencies to address these issues, focusing on support rather than purely disciplinary actions.

## Comprehensive Supervision

Moreover, the framework stipulates that schools must account for students at all times during the school day, not just when they are in classrooms. This encompasses periods during breaks, transitions between lessons, and other times when students may be out of supervised areas. 

Schools are required to establish a written protocol detailing staff responsibilities and notification procedures regarding students who cannot be accounted for. This ensures prompt action is taken if a student does not return to class as expected. Unusual patterns of absence, such as frequent trips to the bathroom or visits to the school clinic, should also be reported to the safeguarding lead if they suggest a potential risk.

### Special Considerations for Vulnerable Students

The framework calls for heightened monitoring of students of determination and those identified as vulnerable. However, it is crucial that any additional supervision is balanced against the need to respect students' basic rights, such as access to essential facilities and care.

In the event of a missing-student incident, schools must act immediately, documenting the situation and escalating it through established safeguarding procedures. This may involve notifying the Child Protection Coordinator or relevant authorities in accordance with Dubai's safeguarding regulations.

## Conclusion

The introduction of these attendance reporting requirements underscores the commitment of Dubai schools to prioritise student safety and wellbeing. For parents, this initiative provides reassurance that schools are actively monitoring attendance and addressing any potential concerns quickly. As these measures are implemented, families can expect a more vigilant approach to safeguarding their children within the educational environment.
