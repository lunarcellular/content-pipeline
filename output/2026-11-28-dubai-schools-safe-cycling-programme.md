## Introduction of Safe Cycling Programme

A new initiative in [Dubai](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools?location=Dubai) [schools](https://www.schoolsery.com/schools) aims to enhance student wellbeing and safety through the introduction of a safe cycling programme as part of physical education classes. The initiative, launched by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), focuses on teaching students how to cycle safely and responsibly, equipping them with essential life skills.

## Overview of the Programme

The Dubai Cyclist Programme encompasses two main components. The first part integrates age-appropriate cycling lessons directly into the physical education curriculum at participating private schools. Here, students will gain essential skills such as bike control, road safety rules, and responsible behaviour on cycling tracks.

The second component allows students to practise their cycling skills in controlled environments that simulate real road conditions. This practical training aims to help students understand potential risks, respect other road users, and build confidence in their cycling abilities.

The programme is designed with inclusivity in mind, ensuring that students of determination can also participate, with activities tailored to accommodate various abilities and learning needs.

## Target and Future Goals

In its initial phase, the programme is expected to reach approximately 6,400 students across Dubai private schools. By 2033, the goal is to expand the programme to benefit over 250,000 students, extending its influence beyond the school environment into families and the wider community.

This initiative aligns with Dubai's broader goals of promoting cycling as a sustainable mode of transport, particularly among school-aged children. It supports various strategic frameworks, including the Education 33 Strategy and the Dubai 2040 Urban Master Plan.

## Collaboration with Government Entities

The programme has garnered support from several governmental bodies, including the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA), Dubai Police, Dubai Municipality, and the Community Development Authority in Dubai (CDA). Their collaboration underscores a collective commitment to fostering a safer and healthier environment for students in Dubai.

According to the RTA, significant infrastructure investments are being made, including the development of over 630 kilometres of safe cycling tracks that will connect residential areas to key destinations and public transport hubs. This infrastructure development aims to facilitate safer cycling practices and encourage families to adopt cycling as a viable mode of transport.

## Importance of Life Skills Education

The introduction of the Dubai Cyclist Programme is a significant step in instilling essential life skills in students. By focusing on safety and responsibility, the programme aims to nurture confident and independent individuals. Early development of such skills is crucial for fostering a generation that values health, wellbeing, and sustainable living.

As Dubai continues to invest in cycling infrastructure and promote a culture of active living, this initiative reflects the emirate's commitment to enhancing the quality of education and the overall wellbeing of its young population.
